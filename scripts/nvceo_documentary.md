## m3_control - The board meeting

Could a board really remove the founder of a company like this? According to Nvidia's latest proxy statement, Huang owns about three point six percent of the company's shares, mostly through family trusts. That is a fortune, but it is far from a majority. The chief executive serves at the pleasure of the board, which CNBC says has eleven directors and a lead director instead of a chair. And removing him as chief executive would not, by itself, remove him from the board. And the proxy statement does not use the word succession even once.

## payoff - The lesson

So why is Nvidia worth so much less than a week ago? Not because its chips changed.
