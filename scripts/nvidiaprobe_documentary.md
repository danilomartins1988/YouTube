## payoff - The lesson

So why did Nvidia lose about four hundred and twenty-eight billion dollars in two days? Not because its chips got worse. Because investors suddenly had to price two risks at once. The first is the risk of depending on one person. The second is the risk that the rules around its most valuable product could bite harder than anyone expected. Neither risk has been shown to have caused any harm. The board says it has no reason to believe secrets were compromised, and nobody has been charged. But markets do not wait for verdicts. They price stories, and they price people. Even a company worth about four trillion dollars can hang on one name, one weekend, and one question.
