## payoff - The lesson

So how does SpaceX actually make money? Not mainly from rockets. The rocket business has been losing money, because SpaceX is pouring billions into Starship, and most launches carry its own satellites. Starlink makes the operating profit. And now the biggest revenue line is artificial intelligence, much of it from renting out computing power, with one unnamed customer bringing in about a third of the latest quarter's revenue. The lesson is that the most famous part of a company is not always the part that pays. At SpaceX, the rocket is the delivery truck, Starlink is the cash register, and the newest business is a landlord with very large bills. Whether that mix keeps working depends on Starship, on a few big customers, and on one man.
