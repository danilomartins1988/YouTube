## m5_grat - The next generation

Wealthy families also use trusts the law allows. One is the grantor retained annuity trust, or GRAT. In nineteen ninety-three, Audrey Walton, from the family of Sam's brother Bud, put about two hundred million dollars of Walmart stock into two short GRATs. The IRS challenged her valuation. In two thousand, the U.S. Tax Court sided with her.

> `grat` [source_located] Walton v. Commissioner, 115 T.C. 589 (Dec 22, 2000): petitioner Audrey J. ## payoff - The lesson

So how did one family keep the world's largest family business? Not with special shares. With timing. Sam Walton gave the company to his children before it was worth much, kept the shares in one block, and let a thin-margin, high-volume machine compound for seventy years. The test now is whether the block holds while pieces of it are sold.
