# Why Are Chinese Cars So Cheap — And So Advanced?

_China's cheap electric cars are mostly the product of an industrial machine (LFP batteries made in-house or next door, clustered suppliers, fast engineering, scale) that years of buyer-focused subsidies helped build; the unsustainable part is the price war, which is squeezing profits and cash so hard that the cars will stay cheap while the number of carmakers shrinks._
