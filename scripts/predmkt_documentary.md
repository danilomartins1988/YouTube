## m1_how - The contract

Start with the product. An event contract is a simple question with a yes or no answer. Will this team win tonight? Will the central bank cut rates next month? If the answer is yes, a yes contract pays one dollar. If the answer is no, it pays nothing.

## m2_formula - The toll

The formula it filed with the regulator in twenty twenty-two looks like this. Seven percent, times the number of contracts, times the price, times one minus the price. At a price of fifty cents, that works out to about one point seven five cents per contract, rounded up to the next cent. There is no fee when the contract settles.

## payoff - The lesson

So how do prediction markets make money off your hunches? Not by betting against you. By charging a small fee every time you trade, most of all when nobody is sure what will happen. Whether that makes them a financial market or a casino is a question the courts are still fighting over. But the toll booth gets paid either way.
