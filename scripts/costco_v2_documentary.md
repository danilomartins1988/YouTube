# How Costco Makes Billions Selling Almost Nothing

_Costco sells almost at cost. The profit hides in a plastic card renewed by nine in ten members, a short list of products sold in enormous volume, a house brand, a dollar-fifty hot dog that keeps people coming back, and the suppliers' money it holds before it pays them._

## co_open - Cold open

Ten in the morning. A giant box with no windows, concrete floors and steel racks up to the ceiling. The doors open, and half the crowd walks right past the televisions, to the back corner, for a hot dog and a soda. Price? a dollar fifty. Reportedly, the same price since nineteen eighty-five.
