## Description (3573 characters, chapters included)

In a single auction, the price to keep enough power plants ready on PJM, America's largest power grid, jumped from $28.92 to $269.92 per megawatt-day, more than nine times higher (PJM; multiple is our calculation). That cost flows into millions of electricity bills. Why are America's power bills rising faster than inflation? Four forces: the wires (utility spending on local distribution up 160% in 20 years), the fuel (gas fell two thirds from 2022 to 2024, yet home prices rose), the data center rush (4.4% of US electricity in 2023, up to 12% by 2028), and a utility business model that earns a return on every new wire.

Every figure is sourced below. 2026 EIA data are preliminary; our own calculations are said as such in the film. Nothing in this video is financial advice. Not affiliated with any utility.
