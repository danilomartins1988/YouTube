On March 8, 2022, the price of nickel on the London Metal Exchange doubled in a few hours and hit $101,365 a tonne. Margin calls that morning reached $19.7 billion. The exchange suspended trading and cancelled about $13.4 billion of trades. What happened that night, why the courts later found the cancellation lawful, and why the UK regulator fined the exchange about $12 million in 2025. Every figure is sourced below, from the FCA final notice and the court judgments.

All amounts are in US dollars; amounts published in pounds were converted at the Federal Reserve H.10 rate.
