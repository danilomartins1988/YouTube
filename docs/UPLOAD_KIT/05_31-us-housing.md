## Description (2732 characters, chapters included)

```
To afford the payments on the typical American home, you now need an income of about $120,800 a year, up from $68,700 five years earlier (Harvard Joint Center for Housing Studies). How did the most ordinary thing in American life slip out of reach? The four forces behind it: the price (almost five years of income), the loan (mortgage rates from 2.65% to above 7%, and the "lock-in" that froze the market), the homes that were never built after 2008, and the zoning rules that decide what can be built at all.
```
