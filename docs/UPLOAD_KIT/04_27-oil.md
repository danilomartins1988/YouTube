## Description (1941 characters, chapters included)

```
On April 20, 2020, the price of the May WTI crude oil contract fell below zero for the first time since the contract began trading in 1983, and settled at -$37.63 a barrel. Sellers were in effect paying buyers to take their oil. How a pandemic demand collapse, a storage hub in Cushing, Oklahoma that was 76% full, and futures contracts that must end in real barrels turned oil into something worth less than nothing, from $0 to -$39.55 in twenty minutes. Every figure is sourced below, mainly from the CFTC staff report, the EIA and the Congressional Research Service.

All amounts are in US dollars as published.
```
