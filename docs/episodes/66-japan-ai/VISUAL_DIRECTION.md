# Visual direction: Will AI Make Japan Rich Again? (Ep 66)

Applied plan: `projects/will-ai-make-japan-rich-again/visual_direction.yaml`. Primary tone trade (warm day), strategy for the robot
superpower and the catch, history for the 1969 plant. Bespoke kit `remotion/src/animation-engine/CartoonStage/japan.tsx` (registered in
CartoonStage.tsx): jtown (Hoshimachi street, railway, shutters, help-wanted sign), jshop (workshop, empty stool, KOBO install and load
cycle), jplatform (crowd thinning), jroad (depot, NO TRUCK pallets), jcare (sensors, lift assist, tea), jhist (sepia 1960s plant),
jfactory (robots building robots, crates to the world or Japan), jdensity, jkonbini (restocking cutaway, remote operator), jdemo
(retired / at work / prototype), jyen, jscale, jtitle, jbumper; items jtag, kobo.
Palette: ORANGE robots, BLUE people, RED shortage and catches, GREEN output and fixes, PURPLE projections and plans, GOLD money.
Signature animations: workforce bars + platform, crates abroad, arms vs androids, Kobo's cycle, per-worker line.
Respect rules: everyday Japan (shophouses, poles and wires, a local train, a vending machine, a convenience store, a care home), no
costumes, no caricatured features, no flags, no logos, day scenes.
