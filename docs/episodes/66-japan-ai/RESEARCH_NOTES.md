# Research notes: Will AI Make Japan Rich Again? (Ep 66), read 2026-11-18

All sources with quotes are in `projects/will-ai-make-japan-rich-again/sources.yaml`. Primary documents were downloaded and read with
pdftotext where possible (IPSS summary PDF, FANUC results PDF, Yaskawa results PDF, IFR WR 2025 foreword PDF, NBER w31914 PDF, Japan
Productivity Center PDF, Cabinet Office CSV).

## Workforce
- Statistics Bureau, Oct 1 2024: 123.80M people; 15-64 = 73.73M (59.6%); 65+ = 36.24M (29.3%).
- IPSS 2023 summary: 15-64 peak 87.26M at the 1995 census; 75.09M (2020); projected 45.35M in 2070 (medium) = 52% of the peak (our calc).
- 2000 census (Statistics Bureau): 86.22M, the first decline since the census began.
- Tokyo ≈ 14.28M (TMG estimate Nov 1, 2025) → the 13.5M drop ≈ "almost all of Tokyo".
- 65+ share 29.6% (Sept 2026), highest among 40M+ countries (Statistics Bureau topics, read via press reports).

## Labour shortages (used only where they serve the story)
- MHLW openings-to-applicants ratio 1.19 (Sept 2026, SA); 1.22 avg 2025 (JILPT table).
- MLIT White Paper 2024: 34% trucking-capacity shortfall in FY2030 without measures; driver overtime cap from April 2024.
- MHLW (Jul 2024): +570,000 care workers needed by FY2040 vs FY2022.

## Robots
- IFR WR 2025 (2024 data): installs 542,076; stock 4.66M; density Korea 1,220, Singapore 818, China 567, Germany 449, Japan 446.
- IFR (Jul 2025): Japan "38% of global robot production".
- Kawasaki: 1969 first industrial robot produced in Japan (Unimation license 1968).
- FANUC FY to Mar 2026: sales ¥857.8bn (≈$5.74bn); robots ¥378.6bn (≈$2.53bn), Japan robot sales down, China up; Japan customers
  ¥110.8bn → 87.1% outside Japan (our calc).
- Yaskawa FY2025 (to Feb 2026): revenue ¥542.1bn; robotics ¥247.0bn (≈$1.65bn), robotics operating profit −14%. (Description only.)
- Exchange rate: Fed G.5A 2025 average 149.5686 JPY/USD (released Jan 5, 2026).

## Real examples (deployment vs demo)
- Deployment: Telexistence restocking arms (FamilyMart since 2021, 7-Eleven pilots 2025), ≈1,000 drinks/day (company claim), remote
  operator takeover.
- Deployment (care): monitoring sensors and lift assists; NBER w33116 (Lee, Iizuka, Eggleston): robot adoption accompanied by more
  employment and retention, more "human touch".
- Construction (not narrated): Shimizu Robo-Welder/Robo-Buddy (AP, 2018) night-shift use; Shimizu itself estimated small savings (~1% of a
  high-rise's cost). Kept out of the film: only 2018 secondary coverage found.
- Demo/plan: Honda ASIMO retired from shows in March 2022 without commercial use (Japan Times); Seven-Eleven Japan + Telexistence humanoid
  "Astra", target 2029 (plan).

## Growth, productivity, counterpoints
- Cabinet Office: real GDP +1.2% (2025), +0.1% (2024).
- JPC 2025 (OECD data): output per hour $60.1 PPP, 28th of 38 (2024).
- NBER w31914: GDP per working-age adult 1998-2019 Japan +31.9% vs US +29.5%.
- Yen: 106.78 (2020) → 149.57 (2025) yen per dollar; −28.6% dollar value of the yen (our calc).
- IMF F&D (Sept 2026): gross debt ≈207% of GDP; net 89–186% depending on assets counted.
