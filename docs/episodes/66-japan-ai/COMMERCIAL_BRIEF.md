## 3. Human question
Hiro can't hire a single worker. Can a robot keep his workshop, and his country, getting richer while it shrinks?

## 4. 30-second hook
Hiro's HELP WANTED sign nobody answers → "His whole country is running out of workers" → whip to a robot plant: "Japan builds almost four
in every ten industrial robots made on Earth" → "could the world's oldest big country turn its biggest problem into its biggest
advantage?" → channel intro.

## 6. Payoff (specific)
AI won't make Japan rich "again" the bubble way (debt and land, see "When Tokyo's Land Was Worth More Than Entire Countries"). Measured per
working-age adult, Japan already grew slightly faster than the US in 1998–2019 (NBER); robots and AI can keep a shrinking Japan getting
richer per person, on three conditions: small firms adopt them (FANUC's home robot sales fell), Japan writes more of the AI brains, and
the debt (≈207% of GDP gross) doesn't crowd it out.
