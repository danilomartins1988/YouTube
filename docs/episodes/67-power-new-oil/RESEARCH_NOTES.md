## The demand side
- IEA, Energy and AI (Apr 10, 2025): data centres ~415 TWh in 2024 → ~945 TWh in 2030 (base case), "slightly more than Japan's total
  electricity consumption today"; AI-optimised centres more than quadruple. FORECAST. Used once (c1_demand).
- IEA commentary on Europe: grid-connection waits 2-10 years by country, 7-10 years in FLAP-D hubs (search excerpt of the IEA page).

## The aluminum precedent (verified)
- Smelting ≈ 14,114 kWh per tonne (IAI world average, 2021, via Statista); US home 10,791 kWh/yr (EIA, 2022) → OUR CALC ≈ 1.3 years.
- Iceland: Landsvirkjun founded 1965, Búrfell on line 1969, built mainly to supply the ISAL smelter at Straumsvík (Landsvirkjun page;
  Alusuisse agreement 1966). Landsvirkjun sells ~85% of its energy to power-intensive industry (customers page). 2024 results hit by
  "historically poor water conditions, leading to a decrease in power sales". NOT used: a second-hand claim that data-centre sales were
  cut to a third of 2022 (DC Market Insights via GCN; no primary).
- Canada (NRCan): 10 primary smelters, 9 in Quebec, 1 in Kitimat; no bauxite mined in Canada.
- Norway: Hydro's five Norwegian smelters run on renewable power (company); hydro ≈ 88% of normal-year production (Ministry of Energy).

## The pins (company disclosures)
- Narvik: Stargate Norway announced Jul 31, 2025 (OpenAI, Nscale, Aker; 230 MW, hydropower and cool climate). Apr 14, 2026 Nscale:
  Microsoft deal, 30,000+ Rubin GPUs at the 230 MW Narvik campus for 2027; Bloomberg reported OpenAI did not conclude a capacity deal
  and the branding was dropped. The film says only "the tenant changed, and Microsoft became the big customer".
- Abilene, Texas: Crusoe, Sept 2025, first two buildings live for Oracle/OpenAI (200 MW+ IT); 1.2 GW committed, expansion dropped Mar
  2026 (Tom's Hardware/Epoch). ERCOT large-load requests ≈ 474 GW (Jul 2026 Senate presentation, via coverage), ~90% data centers,
  63 GW at end-2024; requests are not forecasts. SB 6 (2025): loads ≥75 MW can be told to curtail or run backup in emergencies.
- Gulf: Shuaibah PV 1.04 US¢/kWh (Saudi, 2021), Al Dhafra 1.35 US¢/kWh (Abu Dhabi, 2020): daytime contract prices. UAE-US AI Campus
  up to 5 GW; Stargate UAE 1 GW; first 200 MW expected 2026 (G42 via Gulf News; The National Oct 2026 says still expected this year).
- Quebec: Hydro-Québec 2025 price comparison (rates Apr 1, 2025, excl. taxes), 50 MW customer: Montréal index 100, Boston 445 (≈22%),
  Houston 234; Montréal 5.52 CAD¢/kWh ≈ 3.95 US¢. Hydro-Québec's Feb 19, 2026 filing (R-4333-2026) cites "la fin des surplus
  d'électricité" and proposes Rate CD for data centres ≥5 MW: $32.704/kW + 8.710¢/kWh (proposed text, Jun 29, 2026). OUR CALC at 85%
  load factor: ≈ 14.0 CAD¢ (≈ 10.0 US¢) ≈ 2.5× today's large-power price; BLG says ~13¢ "roughly double"; Le Soleil: would double.
  PROPOSED, decision pending. 5 MW ministerial authorization (2023 law, applied to data-centre supply since 2025; BLG).
- Brazil: renewables 88.2% of electricity (EPE BEN 2025, base 2024); TikTok confirmed its Pecém (Ceará) data center Dec 3, 2025, power
  from new Casa dos Ventos wind; taxes + sector charges 44.8% of electricity-sector gross revenue in 2024 (PwC/Acende Brasil, reported
  Dec 10, 2025); Redata enacted as Law 15,504/2026 (Sept 15, 2026). Investment totals differ by source and are not used. Ep 63's Brazil
  beat is not repeated.
- Ireland: CSO, data centres 22% of metered electricity in 2024 vs urban dwellings 18%; CRU decision (Dec 12, 2025): new data centres
  must bring generation/storage matching their import capacity.
- Norway politics: government said (Jun 20, 2025) it would explore a temporary ban on new proof-of-work crypto-mining data centres.
  Narration: "moved to block new crypto mines"; final legal status not verified.
