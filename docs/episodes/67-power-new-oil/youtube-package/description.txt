AI electricity demand is redrawing the world map. Meet Nia, a site scout hired to find a home for a giant AI data center campus, with one rule from her boss: don't look for workers, look for the plug. A century ago companies hunted for oil. Now they hunt for cheap, abundant electricity. We follow Nia from an old aluminum smelter town to Norway, Iceland, Quebec, Texas, the Gulf and Brazil to answer one question: could cheap electricity become the new oil? (Our answer: yes for where industry lands, with one big difference. Oil can be shipped. Electricity can't, so the winners are the places that can plug you in fast, in big blocks, and are willing to share.)

Key figures (exact values; the film rounds them)
- IEA Energy and AI (Apr 2025), FORECAST: data centers ~415 TWh (2024) → ~945 TWh (2030), slightly more than Japan's total use today.
- Aluminum: ~14,114 kWh per tonne (IAI world average, 2021) vs 10,791 kWh for an average US home (EIA, 2022) ≈ 1.3 years (our calc).
- Iceland: Landsvirkjun (founded 1965) built the Búrfell hydro station (on line 1969) mainly for the ISAL smelter; it sells ~85% of its energy to power-intensive industry; 2024 sales fell on historically poor water conditions.
- Canada: 10 primary aluminum smelters, 9 in Quebec; no bauxite mined in Canada (NRCan).
- Norway: hydro ≈ 88% of normal-year electricity (Ministry of Energy). Narvik campus announced as Stargate Norway (OpenAI, Nscale, Aker; Jul 2025; 230 MW); Apr 2026 Nscale-Microsoft deal for the site (30,000+ GPUs, 2027).
- Quebec: Hydro-Québec 2025 comparison (rates of Apr 1, 2025, excl. taxes; Hydro-Québec is an interested party), 50 MW industrial customer: Montréal 100 vs Boston 445; Montréal 5.52 CAD¢/kWh ≈ 3.95 US¢. PROPOSED data-center Rate CD (filing R-4333-2026): $32.704/kW + 8.710¢/kWh ≈ 14.0 CAD¢ ≈ 10.0 US¢ at 85% load factor ≈ 2.5× (our calc; not approved). Loads of 5 MW+ need ministerial authorization.
- Texas: first two Abilene buildings live Sept 2025 (Crusoe, for Oracle/OpenAI); ERCOT tracked ~474 GW of large-load requests in Jul 2026 (requests, not forecasts; via press coverage); SB 6 (2025) lets ERCOT direct loads of 75 MW+ to curtail in emergencies.
- Gulf: Saudi Shuaibah solar awarded at 1.04 US¢/kWh (2021), Abu Dhabi Al Dhafra 1.35 US¢/kWh (2020): daytime contract prices. PLAN: UAE-US AI Campus up to 5 GW; first 200 MW expected 2026.
- Brazil: 88.2% renewable electricity in 2024 and 86.8% in 2025 (EPE); taxes and sector charges 44.8% of the power sector's gross revenue (PwC / Instituto Acende Brasil, 2024); TikTok confirmed a Pecém, Ceará data center fed by new wind farms (Dec 2025); Redata data-center tax regime, Law 15,504/2026.
- Europe: grid-connection waits for data centers of 2-10 years by country (IEA). Ireland: data centers 22% of metered electricity in 2024 vs 18% for urban homes (CSO); new data centers must bring their own generation or storage (CRU, Dec 2025).
- Norway announced in Jun 2025 it would explore a temporary ban on new proof-of-work crypto-mining data centers (final status not verified).
