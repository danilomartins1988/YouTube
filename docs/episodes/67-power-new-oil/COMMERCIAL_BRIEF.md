## 6. Payoff (specific)
Yes, cheap power is already deciding where the next giant industry lands (Narvik, Abilene, Abu Dhabi, Ceará) exactly as it decided
where aluminum smelters went (Iceland's first dam was built mainly for a smelter; nine of Canada's ten smelters sit in Quebec). But
unlike oil, electricity can't be shipped, so the "new oil" is power you can plug into now, in big blocks, that a government is willing
to share. Winners today: the cold hydro north for training halls, Texas and the Gulf for speed and scale; conditional: Quebec
(permission, a proposed higher data-center rate), Brazil (taxes and charges ≈ 45% of sector revenue); losers: cheap power behind a full
grid (Ireland's Dublin constraint, Europe's waits of up to a decade).
