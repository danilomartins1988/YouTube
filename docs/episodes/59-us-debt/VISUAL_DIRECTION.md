# Visual direction: Who Actually Owns America's $40 Trillion in Debt?

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/who-actually-owns-americas-debt/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** sitcom
2. **Secondary tones:** strategy
3. **Colour palette:** saturated flat primaries, bold outlines (episode palette: bg #F4F1E6, accent #F2B63A, risk #D93A49)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** bright daylight, punchy
5. **Animation intensity:** constant motion, squash and stretch, whip pans
6. **Character behaviour:** warm: explains between gags (demeanour `warm`, at least 4 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** bespoke retro-futurist sets
8. **Charts and diagrams:** bold bars, big numbers
9. **Transitions:** act bumpers, title card
10. **Music and sound:** major turnaround, 3 s chords, bouncy pulse; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - none: one atmosphere throughout
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - No flags, logos, seals or trademarked art; countries appear only as place cards on a neutral dinner table
    - No character, design, name, catchphrase, location, title text or music borrowed from any existing show
    - No public figure drawn (none is central); private individuals are generic characters
    - Jokes never change a number; our calculations, custody caveat and CBO projections labelled on screen
    - Non-partisan (no party, no politician, no blame): the debt is explained, not scored
    - Day scenes, many generic human characters
