## The treasure (verified)
- Power: EPE BEN 2026 summary, "The share of renewables in the electricity matrix was 86.8% in 2025" (2024: 88.2%). Spoken "almost nine
  out of every ten".
- Iron ore: USGS MCS 2026, usable ore 2025e Australia 980 Mt, Brazil 420 Mt (second), India 310 Mt, China 290 Mt; world 2.6 bn t. Brazil
  supplied 58% of US iron ore imports 2021-24 (description only).
- Niobium: USGS MCS 2026, Brazil ≈93% of global production (104,000 t of 112,000 t, 2025e); Canada 5%. CBMM (Araxá, Minas Gerais) calls
  itself the world's leading producer; Fastmarkets/Geopolitical Monitor put its share at ≈77-80%+. Narration: "most of it comes from a single
  mine in my state" (Bia is from Minas Gerais).
- Rare earths: USGS MCS 2026 reserves China 44 Mt, Brazil 11 Mt (second), Australia 6.3 Mt; Brazil mined ≈2,000 t in 2025 (560 t in 2024)
  vs China 270,000 t of 390,000 t. "Barely mines them" is accurate.
- Lithium: USGS MCS 2026, Brazil 12,000 t (2025), one mineral operation (Minas Gerais, Jequitinhonha Valley). No figure spoken.
- Food: USDA FAS Oilseeds: Brazil is the world's largest soybean exporter (2025/26 ≈112-113 Mt forecast). No figure spoken.
- Market: IBGE population estimate 213,421,037 (July 1, 2025).
- Industry capability: Brazilian companies build passenger jets (Embraer) and electric motors exported worldwide (WEG); descriptive, not
  named on screen.

## The backpack (verified, labelled)
- Factories' share: IBGE SIDRA table 1846 (current prices), 2025 Q1-Q4 sums: manufacturing R$1,499,618m of GDP R$12,738,566m = 11.8% (13.7%
  of value added). OUR CALCULATION. 1996 (start of the quarterly series): 13.1% of GDP. CNI: 11.3% in 2018, lowest since 1947. Spoken "about
  one-eighth".
- Taxes: the owner asked for no tax mechanics. The film says only that the bill to produce carries extra weight and some taxes land on top
  of others (cascading), which is what EC 132/2023 targets. No rates spoken.
- Paperwork: World Bank Doing Business 2020 (Paying Taxes): 1,501 hours a year in Brazil (calendar 2018), "the highest time to comply
  globally"; world average 234. The series was discontinued in 2021, so the narration says "the World Bank's last worldwide comparison".
- Logistics: CNT Transport and Logistics Plan 2026 (Jul 30, 2026): road carries 65.8% of Brazil's freight (US ≈43% per CNT). INDUSTRY STUDY.
  World Bank LPI 2023 (latest edition): Brazil score 3.2, rank 51 (description only).
- Credit: Banco Central do Brasil SGS series 432: Selic target 13.75% (read 2026-11-18); Copom held it at 13.75% on Nov 4, 2026, after five
  consecutive cuts from 15%.
- Custo Brasil: MBC (Movimento Brasil Competitivo) with MDIC, FGV update (May 2023): R$1.7 trillion a year more than the OECD average, ≈19.5%
  of GDP; cited by CNI. OUR CONVERSION ≈ US$304 bn. An INDUSTRY ESTIMATE, said as "an estimate" in narration. MDIC/MBC (Nov 2024): six
  initiatives could cut up to R$530 bn in ten years (description only).

## Lightening the load (verified)
- EC 132/2023 (promulgated Dec 20, 2023) and LC 214/2025: dual VAT (federal CBS, state/municipal IBS), non-cumulative, replacing PIS, Cofins,
  IPI (mostly), ICMS and ISS; test 2026 (CBS 0.9%, IBS 0.1%), CBS in full 2027, ICMS/ISS phased down 2029-2032 and extinguished 2033.
  planalto.gov.br refused automated access; read via Agência Senado (as in Ep 63) and PwC Tax Intelligence. Spoken "twenty thirty-three".
- Camaçari: Ford idled the plant in Jan 2021 when it stopped making cars in Brazil; BYD took the site (groundbreaking Oct 2023) and began
  production Jul 1, 2025 (SKD kits first; inauguration Oct 2025; more local stages from 2026). Investment amounts differ by source: not used.
- Redata: Law 15,504/2026 (Sept 15, 2026), renewable/low-emission power condition (same source as Ep 67).
- Rivals: IFR, China is the largest market for industrial robots, installing more than half of new robots worldwide; Mexico's proximity and
  US factory incentives are descriptive.
