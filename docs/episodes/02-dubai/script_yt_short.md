# Dubai Doesn't Make Its Money the Way You Think

_Dubai's own government says more than 95% of its economy is not oil. So what is it?_

## hook

Dubai runs on oil, right? Its own government says more than 95 percent of its economy is not oil at all.

> `non_oil_share` [source_located] The Government of Dubai states that over 95% of Dubai's GDP is non-oil based. (sources: dubai_ae_economy)  

## why

The oil is next door. Abu Dhabi holds about 94 percent of the UAE's reserves. Dubai had to sell something else.

> `reserves_split` [source_located] Abu Dhabi holds about 94% of the UAE's oil reserves and Dubai about 4% (US ITA citing the 2019 UAE State of Energy Report). (sources: trade_gov_uae_energy)  

## reveal

So what pays the bills? Trade is the biggest slice, about a fifth of output. Then finance, real estate and transport.

> `sectors_h1_2026` [source_located] Shares of Dubai's real GDP, H1 2026 (DDSE, revised series): wholesale and retail trade 20.5%, financial and insurance 14.0%, real estate 11.8%, transport and storage 10.7%. (sources: wam_gdp_h1_2026, national_gdp_h1_2026)  

## proof

And people. 95.2 million passengers passed through its airport in 2025.

> `dxb_pax_2025` [source_located] Dubai International Airport (DXB) handled 95.2 million passengers in 2025. (sources: dmo_dxb_2025)  

## outro

Dubai doesn't sell oil. It sells a place to meet. More on The Hidden Economics.
