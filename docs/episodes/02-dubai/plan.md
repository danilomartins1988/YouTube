# Episode 02 - "How Dubai Really Makes Money": outline, research and production plan

Status: **awaiting editor approval** (nothing rendered, nothing spent). Prepared 2026-11-03.

Central question: *How did Dubai turn itself into a global centre for trade, aviation, tourism, real
estate and finance, and what actually drives its economy today?*

Thesis (working): **Dubai's product is connection.** A small oil endowment was spent early on
permanent infrastructure (creek, ports, airport); state-owned companies (DP World, Emirates, ICD,
Dubai Holding) built hubs ahead of demand; light-touch rules, free zones and low taxes pulled in
foreign capital and people. The model is powerful and fragile for the same reason: it depends on
other people's money, goods and workers continuing to flow through.
