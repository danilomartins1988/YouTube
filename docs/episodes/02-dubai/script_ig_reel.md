# How Dubai Became a Global Business Hub

_From 19 companies to more than 11,000 - how Dubai sells rules, not resources._

## hook

In 1985, this Dubai free zone had nineteen companies. Today it has more than 11,000.

> `jafza_firms` [source_located] Jebel Ali Free Zone (Jafza), established in 1985, started with 19 companies and now hosts over 11,000 businesses from 157 countries. (sources: dmo_jafza_2025)  

## rules

The trick was rules. Free zones offered full foreign ownership, and Since 2021, so does most of the mainland.

> `onshore_ownership` [source_located] Since 1 June 2021, foreign investors can own 100% of most onshore UAE companies, after the amendment of the Commercial Companies Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2020). (sources: wam_ownership_2021)  
> `free_zones` [source_located] Dubai has more than 20 free zones (Invest in Dubai). (sources: invest_dubai_hub)  
> "Most of the mainland": most onshore activities, per the 2021 Commercial Companies Law amendment.  

## courts

For finance, Dubai built a zone with its own common-law courts. The DIFC now has 8,844 active companies.

> `difc_courts` [source_located] The DIFC, created under Dubai law in 2004, has its own English-language common-law courts. (sources: difc_courts_framework)  
> `difc_2025` [source_located] At the end of 2025 the DIFC had 8,844 active companies and a workforce of 50,200, including more than 290 banks and capital-markets firms. (sources: dmo_difc_2025)  

## tax

It isn't tax-free any more. Most firms pay 9 percent corporate tax, but qualifying free-zone income can still pay zero percent.

> `ct_rate` [source_located] The UAE corporate tax rate is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 (0% below), for financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023. (sources: uae_ct, mof_tax)  
> `qfzp` [source_located] A Qualifying Free Zone Person pays 0% corporate tax on qualifying income and 9% on other income, subject to substance and other conditions. (sources: fta_qfzp)  

## outro

Dubai turned its rulebook into a product.
