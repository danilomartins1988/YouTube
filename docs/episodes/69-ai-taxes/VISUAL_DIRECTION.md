# Visual direction: If AI Replaces Workers, Who Pays the Taxes? (Ep 69)

Applied file: `projects/if-ai-replaces-workers-who-pays-the-taxes/visual_direction.yaml`. Prime-time TV-cartoon energy at GENRE level only
(thick outlines, flat colour, squash and stretch); everything original. Day scenes, human characters, a bespoke world.

- **Kit:** `remotion/src/animation-engine/CartoonStage/aitaxes.tsx` (new file; registered in CartoonStage.tsx with a small edit).
  Sets: txtown (Pinewick Main Street: school, town hall clock, shops that shutter, potholes, school bus, warehouse on the hill), txware
  (warehouse floor, robot arms, empty lockers, party confetti), txpipes (money plumbing: paycheck tap, profits tap, the tank, SCHOOLS /
  ROADS / SOCIAL SECURITY, new taps for proposals), txfair (the idea fair, four booths, ferris wheel), txscale (the Fair Scale: GOOD
  REASON vs THE CATCH), txjars (tax jars of rich democracies; landscapes, no flags), txalaska (snow, pipeline, the fund, flying checks,
  barrel → robot head), txmail (houses and mailboxes for the cash pilots), txclock (Social Security office, reserve tank, 2034
  calendar), txmeeting (town meeting, hands up), txtitle, txbumper. Items: stub (walking pay stub that tears slices off), rivet (robot
  with pockets gag and a raised hand).
- **Cast (fictional):** ROSA (mayor), STUB (Ray's last pay stub), RIVET (robot); Ray is mentioned, not shown. No public figure drawn;
  Bill Gates is quoted in narration only; no politicians as characters; no logos, no flags.
- **Presenter boards:** p10 (the tank built for the big tap), p24 (who carries the bill), p35 (the whole puzzle on one page).
- **Numbers on screen:** one big number at a time, always with a claim pill (STORY / SCENARIO / OUR CALC / PROJECTION / FACT / FINDING /
  QUOTE / LIBRARY / VERDICT).
- **Palette meaning:** BLUE people's taxes, TEAL profits, GOLD money/reserve, GREEN good reason, RED catch, ORANGE robots.
