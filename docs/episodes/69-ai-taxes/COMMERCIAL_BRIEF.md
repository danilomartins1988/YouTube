## 6. Payoff (specific)
Today the bill sits mostly on workers (≈86% of FY2026 federal receipts from individual income and payroll taxes; corporate ≈7%). If work
shrinks, the bill has to move: a robot tax (Korea's 2017 trimmed credit; Europe said no), taxing owners (profits can travel), a shopping
tax (hard to dodge, harder on tight budgets), an AI dividend (Alaska shows it works with a fund you already own) or basic income
(Stockton: more full-time work; the larger OpenResearch trial: 1-2 fewer hours a week, no better jobs). Nobody has decided; each fix
chooses whose shoulders carry the bill, and Social Security's 2034 clock means the debate arrives before the robots do.
