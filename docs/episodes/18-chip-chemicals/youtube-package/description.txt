Everyone talks about the machines that make chips. But the most advanced chip factory on Earth can be stopped by a bottle of liquid, a tank of gas or a silver metal. Three Japanese companies make about 90% of the resist used for EUV chips (an estimate), Ukraine once purified about half of the world's chip-grade neon, and China produces 99% of the world's raw gallium. How each chokepoint was used, and what governments are doing about it. Every figure is sourced below; estimates are labelled.
