# Visual direction: The Three Companies That Decide How Much Your Memory Costs

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/the-three-companies-that-decide-memory-prices/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** crisis
2. **Secondary tones:** strategy, tech, reflective
3. **Colour palette:** storm reds and bruised purples, high contrast (episode palette: bg #140C16, accent #FFC21A, risk #FF4B3E)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** dusk, alarm pulse growing with the crisis
5. **Animation intensity:** accelerating; shakes and fast camera at the breaking points
6. **Character behaviour:** restrained: draws cascades and contagion, no jokes (demeanour `neutral`, at least 3 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** trading floors, bank fronts, streets with queues; storm skies
8. **Charts and diagrams:** steep falls, warning indicators, contagion networks
9. **Transitions:** hard cuts, hits
10. **Music and sound:** D-minor progression, faster pulse, low drone; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "Three companies": crisis → strategy
    - chapter "The customer who pays more": strategy → tech
    - chapter "The challenger": tech → strategy
    - chapter "Boom and bust": strategy → crisis
    - chapter "The lesson": crisis → reflective
    - inside `m3_squeeze` at "curtailed": → crisis (the AI story lands back on the consumer)
    - inside `m5_next` at "new factories are coming": → strategy (the cycle turns: supply is on its way)
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - One memory stick on a shop shelf is the through-line: price tag flips $8.70 -> $48
    - Three company towers / factories as an oligopoly, never logos (names as text)
    - HBM: AI chip with stacked memory, wafer split diagram
    - CXMT: a Shanghai trading board debut (STAR market) with restrictions shown as a barrier
    - Boom and bust: rollercoaster price chart, factories arriving at once
    - No smiling sun, no generic colourful city; shop, fab, data centre, exchange, boardroom only
