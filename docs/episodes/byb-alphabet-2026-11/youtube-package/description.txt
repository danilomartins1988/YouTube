Alphabet is worth about $3.52 trillion across all its share classes, about 178 times its last twelve months of free cash flow by our calculation, after $159.8 billion of capital spending. Free cash flow was negative in each of the last two quarters, and this year Alphabet sold new stock instead of buying any back. So what is that price actually betting on? In this edition of Before You Buy, we open Alphabet's money machine (Google Services, Search, Google Cloud and the TPU systems, the Other Bets), follow the cash into data centers, new shares and new debt, run three lenses and a simple reverse calculation on a labelled proxy and on free cash flow, ask what each dollar of capex has to earn, weigh what has to go right, what could go wrong and the upside Alphabet itself points to, and finish with the three numbers to watch at the next report.
