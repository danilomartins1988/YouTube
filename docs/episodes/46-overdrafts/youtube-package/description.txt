The typical overdraft fee at America's largest banks is $35 (CFPB), and the median debit card purchase that triggers one was just $24. Banks with more than $1 billion in assets still collected $5.83 billion in overdraft and bounced-payment fees in 2023, down from $11.96 billion in 2019. So why is a fee everyone hates still worth billions? We explain how an overdraft is made, why about 9% of accounts paid almost 79% of the fees in a CFPB study, how the big banks cut fees in 2021–22, how a federal $5 fee option was written in December 2024 and erased by Congress in May 2025, and what bank reports show since.
