Tesla is worth about $1.5 trillion, about 466 times its last twelve months of profit by our calculation, and about a third of its operating profit came from regulatory credits sold to other carmakers. So what is that price actually betting on? In this edition of Before You Buy, we open Tesla's money machine (cars, credits, services and energy storage), follow the cash and the share count, run three lenses, show why a reverse calculation breaks at a 0.2% free cash flow yield, use a steady-state cash lens instead, weigh what has to go right (autonomy, energy, robots, cars without credits), what could go wrong and the upside Tesla itself points to, and finish with the three numbers to watch at the next report.
