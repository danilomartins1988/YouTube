At AutoNation, one of America's largest dealership groups, the average new car sold in 2025 earned $2,564 of gross profit. The finance office earned $2,769 on the same deal (AutoNation 10-K). So where does a car dealership really make its money? Four engines: the showroom (new cars: 49% of revenue, 13% of gross profit), the finance office (loans, warranties and add-ons: 5% of revenue, 30% of gross profit), the service bay (17% of revenue, 48% of gross profit), and the state franchise laws that keep carmakers from selling directly to you. Plus the buyer's bill: $1.71 trillion in auto loans and payments of about $767 a month.

Every figure is sourced below. The profit mix is from one public dealer group (AutoNation) and our own calculations are said as such. Nothing in this video is financial advice. Not affiliated with any dealer or carmaker.
