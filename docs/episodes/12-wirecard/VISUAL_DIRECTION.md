# Visual direction: The $2 Billion That Never Existed

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/the-2-billion-that-never-existed/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** thriller
2. **Secondary tones:** strategy, crisis, reflective
3. **Colour palette:** slate, ink blue, desaturated; gold for money, red for the lie (episode palette: bg #0F1520, accent #D9A441, risk #E0473F)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** low-key, one pool of light on the evidence, heavy vignette, film grain
5. **Animation intensity:** slow, deliberate; moments of stillness before revelations
6. **Character behaviour:** serious: examines documents, connects evidence, points at gaps; never smiles or hops (demeanour `serious`, at least 3 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** night offices, archives, vaults, courtrooms; no smiling sun, no clouds
8. **Charts and diagrams:** thin lines on dark screens, red highlights on inconsistencies
9. **Transitions:** slow cuts, scan lines, stamps
10. **Music and sound:** low A-minor pad with E-major tension, heartbeat pulse, 55 Hz drone; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "The rocket": thriller → strategy
    - chapter "The warnings": strategy → thriller
    - chapter "The collapse": thriller → crisis
    - chapter "Who failed": crisis → thriller
    - chapter "The lesson": thriller → reflective
    - inside `m1_dax` at "At its peak": → thriller (the peak of the rocket is where the questions begin)
    - inside `m3_banks` at "most likely did not exist": → crisis (the admission: the mystery becomes a collapse)
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - Opening: dark office, one lit document, the missing-money question; no sky decorations
    - The rocket: brighter corporate imagery and rising charts (strategy), darkening at the peak
    - Warnings / money: documents, stamps, magnifier on inconsistencies; spotlight on the evidence
    - Collapse: fast deterioration, alarm pulse, storm; accurate institutional consequences
    - Lesson: calm, low light, the empty vault revisited; presenter serious, no smile
