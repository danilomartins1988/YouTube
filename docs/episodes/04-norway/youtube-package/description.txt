Norway found oil in 1969 - but plenty of countries have oil. What made Norway different is what it decided to do with it: own it, tax it, save it abroad, and spend only the expected return. The North Sea discovery, Statoil and the 78% petroleum tax, the Government Pension Fund Global and how it invests, the fiscal rule, Norway's productivity and social model - and the risks ahead. Every figure is sourced below. All amounts are in US dollars, converted from kroner at the Federal Reserve H.10 annual average of each year (2026 figures at the 2025 average).
