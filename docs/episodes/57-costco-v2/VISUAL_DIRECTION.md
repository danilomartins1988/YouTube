# Ep 57 visual direction: How Costco Makes Billions Selling Almost Nothing

Applied plan: `projects/how-costco-makes-billions-selling-almost-nothing/visual_direction.yaml` (director's edit).

1. **Primary tone:** `sitcom` (owner: "as animated as possible", prime-time animated TV sitcom, genre level only). Secondary:
   `history` for the 1983 Seattle flashback (a1_size), with a tone shift back to sitcom at "Net sales grew".
2. **Palette:** warm warehouse cream `#FFF6E8`, ink `#1B2433`, safety orange `#FF9F1C` (money/highlights), blue `#2F6FD1`
   (membership), green `#2E9E5B` (savings), red `#E53950` (costs, the $1.50 price, risks).
3. **TV structure:** cold open (10 a.m. rush past the TVs to the food court) → channel intro as the show's "theme" →
   title card "THE CLUB" with typed subtitle "Tonight: Membership Has Its Pallets" → five acts, each with a bumper (forklift crate
   with the act number, joke caption) → payoff → end card → button.
4. **Running gag:** the food-court board says $1.50; in every bumper someone tries to change it (ladder, crane, drone,
   sticker, robot arm), a buzzer sounds, "NOPE."; the button: "NEW PRICE, FINALLY!" ... "STILL $1.50".
5. **Cast (original):** BEV (membership clerk), HANK (bulk-buying dad), PEARL (sample lady), SKIP (food-court teen), CARDIE
   (walking membership card with an LED face), LIFT (forklift). Generic shoppers otherwise.
6. **Real people:** Ron Vachris (CEO, short grey hair, navy blazer, holding his shareholder letter) in a3_kirk; Jim Sinegal
   (co-founder, white hair, shirt-sleeves, plain "JIM" tag, holding a hot dog) in a4_sinegal while his attributed quote plays.
   Name labels on first appearance; nothing else attributed to them; no mockery.
7. **Bespoke sets (`remotion/src/animation-engine/CartoonStage/costco.tsx`, day scenes):** clubfloor, memberdesk,
   foodcourt, housebrand, treasureaisle, loadingdock, gasbar, seattle83, cashvault, clubtitle, clubbumper.
8. **Signature animations:** the cold-open stampede to the food court; the $100 receipt; the membership desk card printer
   and renewal screen with the queue of returning members; the treasure-hunt turntable whose item keeps changing; the
   house-brand labeller; the loading-dock truck and day clock; the dividend vault and coin chute.
9. **Presenter boards (5):** $100 split (bars), fee history (bars), the squeeze (boxes + arrows), the cash clock
   (timeline), the machine loop (boxes + arrows).
10. **Charts:** only sourced data; our calculations labelled "OUR CALCULATION"; the fee-history bars say they are not
    drawn from zero.
11. **Sound:** sitcom music mood; hits on bumpers, the buzzer beat at 2.3 s, "SAFE!" and the dividend.
12. **Requirements:** no Costco logo, typeface, store design or card design; names in plain text only; jokes never change a
    number.

Thumbnail: face concept F (Ron Vachris bust, "COSTCO'S / REAL PRODUCT", $1.50 hot dog prop) is the default; standard
concepts A ("COSTCO'S / $1.50 SECRET", hot dog + member card) and B ("COSTCO / SELLS ACCESS", hot dog vs card).
