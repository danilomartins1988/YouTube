## Verified figures ($ millions unless noted; FY2026 / FY2025)
- Net sales 297,247 / 269,912 (+10.1%); membership fees 5,907 / 5,323 (+11%; FY2024 4,828; FY2017 2,853); total revenue
  303,154; merchandise costs 264,279; SG&A 27,190; operating income 11,685 / 10,383; net income 9,226 / 8,099.
- Gross margin percentage 11.09% / 11.12% (10.92% FY2024). "significantly lower gross margins ... than most other retailers".
- Paid members 84.1M (81.0M); household cards 66.3M; cardholders 150.4M; Executive 42.3M (38.7M; 35.4M FY2024), ≈ 75.2% of net

- Deferred membership fees on the balance sheet: 3,006 (Aug 30, 2026); fees recognised ratably over the membership year.
- "less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse"; 939 warehouses at FYE (647 U.S. & PR); 945 on Nov 10, 2026.
- Pallets: "stored on racks above the sales floor and displayed on pallets containing large quantities, reducing labor required".
- Kirkland Signature ≈ $90B annual revenue globally; 15-20% savings vs national brand; 30th trademark anniversary in 2025

  taking advantage of early payment discounts."
- 774 gas stations; gasoline = 11% of net sales (FY2026).
- 355,000 employees (FY2026); average U.S. hourly wage ≈ $32 (2025 letter); pay = "largest expense after the cost of merchandise".
- Risk factor: "Membership loyalty and growth are essential to our business."

## Our calculations (labelled on screen)
- Fees / operating income = 50.6% ("equal to about half"; fees have their own costs).
- Per $100 of net sales: $88.91 goods, $11.09 gross margin, $9.15 SG&A, $1.94 left before membership fees ("about two dollars").
- Markup over cost = 32,968 / 264,279 = 12.5%.
- Executive share of paid members = 50.3%. Fees FY2026 / FY2017 = 2.07x ("more than double").
- Kirkland $90B / FY2025 net sales $269.9B ≈ 33% ("about a third", rough: company statement vs reported sales).
- Inventory turns = 264,279 / 18,720 ≈ 14.1 a year (≈ 26 days). Accounts payable = 117% of inventory.
- Special dividend / FY2026 fees = 13.3 / 5.907 = 2.25 ("more than two years of membership fees").
