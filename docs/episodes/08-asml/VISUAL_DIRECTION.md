2. **Secondary tones:** strategy
3. **Colour palette:** deep navy, cyan glow, white; precise (episode palette: bg #081226, accent #3FD0FF, risk #FF5A6E)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** dark with cyan glow and a soft spotlight
5. **Animation intensity:** precise, layered builds
6. **Character behaviour:** neutral: assembles components, draws cutaways (demeanour `neutral`, at least 4 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** clean rooms, machines, data centres
8. **Charts and diagrams:** layered component diagrams, glowing networks
9. **Transitions:** scan lines, zoom-ins
10. **Music and sound:** E-minor pad with a high shimmer; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "How one company ended up alone": tech → strategy
    - chapter "Who builds it": strategy → tech
    - chapter "Where the machine may go": tech → strategy
    - chapter "The lesson": strategy → tech
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - Machine scenes: dark clean room, cyan glow, layered cutaways of light source and mirrors
    - Business chapters: cool strategic diagrams (strategy tone)
    - Presenter neutral: assembles components, draws the supply network
    - No sky decorations inside the fab
