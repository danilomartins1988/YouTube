## Description (4138 chars, YouTube limit 5,000)

```
The typical existing home in America sold for $416,700 in October 2026 (NAR). The typical household earned a record $87,460 in 2025 (Census). With 30-year mortgage rates above 7% (Freddie Mac), the paycheck you need has run past the paycheck most people have. We follow it like an episode of an animated sitcom: a home that now costs almost five years of income, the rate rollercoaster that added about $900 a month to the same house, the owners locked in by cheap older loans, a decade of homes America never built, the zoning board that decides what may be built at all, and the first-time buyer who is now 40.

Every figure is sourced below: the National Association of Realtors (October 2026 existing-home sales, the Housing Affordability Index, the 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers), Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey, the U.S. Census Bureau, Harvard's Joint Center for Housing Studies, an FHFA working paper on mortgage lock-in, Up for Growth, the National Association of Home Builders and UC Berkeley. Values marked "our calculation" are our own arithmetic (principal and interest only); housing-shortage numbers are estimates and vary by method; the zoning share is for Greater Los Angeles. Maya, Rex and DeeDee are fictional characters; the homes, towns and signs are original. Not affiliated with any realtor, lender or builder. Not financial advice.
```
