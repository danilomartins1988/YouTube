# Visual direction: Why Americans Can No Longer Afford a Home (Ep 58)

Director's plan; the YAML twin (`projects/why-americans-can-no-longer-afford-a-home/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the
render (palette, scene tones, presenter demeanour, music bed).

1. **Primary emotional tone:** sitcom (prime-time animated comedy, genre level only; all original), secondary strategy.
2. **Format:** cold open (Saturday open house, bidding war) → channel intro as the "theme" → title card "STARTER HOME"
   ("Tonight: Three Bedrooms, One Bath, Forty Bidders") → five acts with bumpers → the basement → end card → button joke
   (Maya gets the keys... to the basement; "RENT: $0 · LANDLORD: MOM").
3. **Running gag:** each bumper's STARTER HOME shrinks (bungalow → shed → doghouse → birdhouse → mailbox) while Maya runs
   at it and DeeDee beats her to it with a SOLD sign.
4. **Cast (fictional):** MAYA (first-time buyer), REX (her dad, refinanced near the bottom, locked in), DEEDEE (realtor);
   generic buyers and neighbours. No real people.
5. **Bespoke sets (CartoonStage/housing.tsx):** openhouse, ratecoaster (the track is the Freddie Mac weekly 30-year rate on
   a real % × year grid), lockstreet (padlocked homes), shrinker (SHRINK-O-MATIC with an "affordable listings" gauge),
   emptylot (a frame that builds, stalls, and gets tumbleweeds), zoningboard (dais, gavel, wall clock, parcel map that turns
   yellow, protest signs), basement.
6. **Signature animations:** the bidding war (s01), the rate rollercoaster (s11), the padlock street (s17), the
   Shrink-O-Matic (s08), the zoning map (s28), the stalled lot (s22).
7. **Presenter boards (4):** years-of-income bars (p09), the income test with a gap bracket (p13), why shortage estimates
   differ, 2 boxes + arrow (p24), one lot / duplex / fourplex (p30, illustrative).
8. **Palette:** warm day cream, gold accent, blue = rates/data, green = supply, red = cost/risk.
9. **Camera:** whip pans with speed lines on punchlines, a tour of five worlds in the promise, the camera rides the coaster.
10. **Labels:** OUR CALCULATION (payments, income needed, decade sums), ESTIMATES (shortage, NAHB, FHFA), ONE REGION (Greater LA),
    ILLUSTRATIVE (one-lot drawing).
11. **Music and sound:** sitcom bed; hits on SOLD, CLANK and BAM; whooshes on whip pans.
12. **Thumbnail:** face concept (Maya bust, shocked, next to a $416,700 tag; "CAN'T AFFORD A HOME") as default, plus a
    standard bignum concept (padlocked starter house; "HOMES OUT OF REACH").
