## 3. Human question
Nvidia's sales just doubled, so why does the stock cost only about sixteen times its profit, and what would have to go
right for that price to make sense?

## 4. First 30 seconds

just fallen ≈ 22% in two weeks, with the founder-CEO's resignation accepted on Nov 11 → "what is this price betting on?"
→ disclaimer → channel intro after the machine section (series layout).

## 7. Payoff
The price does not pay for today's growth: it implies roughly 5.6-10.3% a year of free-cash-flow growth (our
illustration), i.e. the market prices the boom like a peak. The test is whether margins, collections and the big buyers
hold through the Rubin ramp; the three scorecard numbers say so next quarter.
