## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
Two hundred billion dollars. That is roughly what four companies spent on buildings and machines in just three months. Microsoft. Alphabet, the owner of Google. Amazon. And Meta, the owner of Facebook and Instagram. Much of that money went into one thing. Data centers, packed with chips for artificial intelligence. Spending like that has to come back somehow. So how does a chatbot, an AI model, or a data center turn into actual money?

So how does Big Tech really make money from AI? By renting the machines, by selling better ads, and by locking in customers for years. The chatbot is the shop window. The real business is behind it, in the data centers, in the ad auctions, and in contracts that will pay out long after the next model is released.
