# Ep 63 visual direction

Applied plan: `projects/could-ai-bring-europe-back-to-power/visual_direction.yaml`. Storyboard generator: `tools/gen_europeai_storyboard.py`
(47 scenes). Bespoke kit: `remotion/src/animation-engine/CartoonStage/europe.tsx` (registered in `CartoonStage.tsx` with one import and three
spreads). Thumbnail subject: `eurocity` in `remotion/src/thumbnails/CinematicThumb.tsx`.

- **Tone:** premium documentary (strategy palette, calm camera moves, short whips only on reveals) in the channel's prime-time cartoon genre:
  thick clean outlines, flat colour, original cast and sets, human characters, day scenes.
- **Cast (fictional):** NORA (researcher, glasses) and THEO (factory engineer). The recurring presenter draws six boards: the size gap, the
  growth formula, the cost stack, the medicine value chain, Company E, and the comeback conditions. A drafting-pen mascot (compasspen)
  underlines bumpers and the button.
- **Worlds:** eurocity (old works, old town, new labs that rise with `era`), triad (USA / EU / CHINA plinths, robot-density bars), agewave
  (workers vs retiree balance), squeeze (factory in an ENERGY / TECH GAP vise), smartfab (automated factory: vision gantry, predictive
  maintenance, carts, digital twin), pharmachain (lab -> patents -> trials -> factory -> shipping -> pharmacies, money flowing back), medplaza
  (six HQ towers with city plaques and an EU rope; EU port with export ships), lithoweb (an original lithography machine between suppliers
  and chip fabs), twinfab (factory + holographic twin shipping automation worldwide), jetyard (a generic airliner joined from parts),
  ownmap (European campus vs partner factories), profitlab (before/after columns per $100), cleanpower (dam, wind and solar feeding a robot
  plant; power-bill column), magnet (capital magnet over a generic continent; risk signs), eutitle, eubumper.
- **Labels:** every number carries an epitag pill (FACT / MECHANISM / SCENARIO / CONJECTURE / HYPOTHESIS / ILLUSTRATIVE); the experiment shows
  ILLUSTRATIVE MODEL · MADE-UP NUMBERS; Company E shows HYPOTHETICAL; the comeback city shows SCENARIO · NOT A FORECAST.
- **Originality and rights:** no logos, flags, traced landmarks or traced products; companies appear as generic buildings or machines with a
  city plaque; no real person drawn (Draghi and Busch are named in narration and captions only).
