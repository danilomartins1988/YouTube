Quick guide to what's fact and what's not in this film:
FACT = sourced and dated (IMF, Eurostat, the Draghi report, IFR, EFPIA/Eurostat, company annual reports, EPE).
MECHANISM = how the economics works, not a forecast.
SCENARIO / HYPOTHESIS = a possible path, clearly not a prediction.
ILLUSTRATIVE = Company E and its numbers are made up to show the logic.

All money is in US dollars; euro, krone, franc and pound figures were converted at ECB average rates (our conversion).
