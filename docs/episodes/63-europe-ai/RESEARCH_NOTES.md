# Ep 63 research dossier: Could AI Bring Europe Back to Power?

All pages read on 2026-11-18 unless noted. Machine-readable sources and claims: `projects/could-ai-bring-europe-back-to-power/sources.yaml`
(status `source_located`; a named human reviewer still has to sign off). USD only; every conversion labelled "our conversion".

## Scope and conversion rules
- **EU** = the 27 member states (IMF, Eurostat, Draghi report, IFR "EU" density, Eurostat COMEXT trade). **Europe** = the continent
  (IFR "Europe" installations; EFPIA totals include Switzerland, the UK, Norway, Turkey). The **eurozone** is not used for any
  on-screen figure (IMF euro-area GDP 2025 = $18.0T is recorded here only).
- UK (AstraZeneca, GSK) and Switzerland (Roche, Novartis) are in Europe, **not in the EU**; the film says so explicitly (a4_six) and the
  plaza scene ropes off only Paris and Bagsværd as EU.
- FX: ECB 2025 average reference rates via Bank of Finland: per EUR USD 1.1300, DKK 7.4634, CHF 0.9370, GBP 0.85687. Cross rates (our
  calculation): USD per DKK 0.15141, per CHF 1.20598, per GBP 1.31875. Siemens fiscal 2026 (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026): mean of 255 ECB
  daily USD reference rates = 1.1616 (ECB Data Portal, series EXR.D.USD.EUR.SP00.A; our calculation).

## Act 3: automation (facts)
- IFR World Robotics 2026 (24 Sep 2026; data 2025): Europe installed 73,054 industrial robots (-14%); China 354,226; US 38,428; world
  603,307. Machine vision "already established and industry-grade"; predictive maintenance, process optimization and AI-based inspection
  "increasingly used"; general-purpose physical AI "not yet industry-grade".
- IFR density release (12 Nov 2026; data 2025), robots per 10,000 manufacturing employees: Germany 469, US 323, EU 240, China 193 (new
  employment base, not compared with older editions), world 152.

## Act 6: owning vs operating
- Illustrative model (no claim): per $100 of sales; contract manufacturer costs 60 -> 50 after automation; fee = cost + 5% (3.00 -> 2.50);
  Company E's own R&D and sales costs 20; Company E residual 17 -> 27.50; group operating profit 20 -> 30. Assumes prices hold and Company E
  genuinely performs and controls development and risk (see FACT_CHECK.md for the OECD framing).
- Owner-added beat (Brazil, scenario): EPE Brazilian Energy Balance 2026: renewables = 86.8% of the electricity matrix in 2025 (88.2% in
  2024). PwC / Instituto Acende Brasil (2025): in 2024 taxes (30%) and sector charges (14.8%) = 44.8% of the gross operating revenue of 44
  power companies (~2/3 of the market). Constitutional Amendment 132/2023: dual VAT (CBS, IBS) replacing PIS, Cofins, IPI, ICMS and ISS,
  test collection 2026, transition to 2033 (Agência Senado). No BRL figure is shown, so no BRL conversion was needed.
  The owner's brief suggested 88-90% renewable; the latest official figure (2025) is 86.8%, which the film uses.
