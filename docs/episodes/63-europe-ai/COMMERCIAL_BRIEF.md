## Owner-added beat (2026-11-18): cheap energy as a location factor (Act 6, ~45 s)
When labor's share of cost shrinks, energy, the cost of money, logistics, stability and the taxes and charges on producing become bigger
location factors. Brazil example (facts): 86.8% renewable electricity in 2025 (EPE); taxes and sector charges ~45% of power companies'
2024 gross revenue (PwC / Instituto Acende Brasil); a dual VAT phased in 2026-2033 (EC 132/2023). Scenario: an energy-rich country would
need competitive costs to produce, energy charges and taxes included; even then the technology owner (possibly European) can capture the
value. Labelled SCENARIO; no policy recommendation.

## Payoff
AI does not hand Europe the factory race (that is China's) and Europe will not copy Silicon Valley. The plausible path is as the owner,
developer, financier and licensor of technology the world runs on, as its drugmakers, ASML, Siemens and Airbus already are, provided it
scales companies at home, eases capital, cuts energy costs, adopts AI and wins talent. "A plausible path, not a promise."
