## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
One hundred and sixty billion dollars. That is how much interest Americans were charged on their credit cards in a single year. And that is only one side of the business. Every time you tap your card, even if you pay your bill in full, the store you are buying from pays a fee too. You just never see it. So who really makes money from that small piece of plastic in your wallet?

## 7. Payoff (as rendered)
So what is the hidden business behind your credit card? It is not the plastic. It is the position. The card sits between you and every store, and every company in that chain takes a slice. If you pay in full, the system can work in your favor. If you carry a balance, you are the one paying for it. Either way, the card is never really free.
