## Sources and verified claims
- **NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Q2 2026** (2026-08-11), https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/news/research/2026/20260811
  - "Credit card balances rose by $21 billion and stood at $1.26 trillion."
  - Flow into serious delinquency (90+) 6.97%.
- **NY Fed Liberty Street** (2026-08-11), https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/08/how-distressed-are-consumers-reconciling-diverging-credit-card-delinquency-measures/
  - 90+ delinquent share of balances rose from 7.6% to 12.8% (2022Q3 to 2026Q1).
  - "more than 23 million Americans still carrying charged-off credit card balances".
- **Fed G.19** (2026-11-06), https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/current/default.htm
  - APR on accounts assessed interest: 22.36% (2026Q3, preliminary).
  - Annual: 2021 16.45%; 2024 22.89%.
  - 2015 ≈ 13.5–13.9% (from the FRED series TERMCBCCINTNS; a data series, not a quote).
- **CFPB blog** (2024-02-22), https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/blog/credit-card-interest-rate-margins-at-all-time-high/
  - APR margin 14.3%, "the highest point in recent history".
  - +4.3 percentage points from 2013 to 2023.
  - "estimated $25 billion in additional interest revenue".
- **CFPB, Consumer Credit Card Market report** (Dec 2025), https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-credit-card-market-report_2025.pdf
  - Margin 16.4% in 2024 (a different dataset from the blog; do not chain the two).
  - "$160 billion in interest charges" in 2024, up from $105 billion in 2022.
  - Late fees $17.0bn in 2024 (+17% since 2022); "$31.3 billion in fees".
  - About 50% of accounts revolve.
  - 92% of general-purpose spending is on rewards cards.
- **CFPB, Consumer Credit Card Market report** (Oct 2023), https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-credit-card-market-report_2023.pdf
  - "Issuers typically fund rewards through interchange revenue."
  - Average interchange rate 1.8%; rewards are almost nine-tenths of interchange revenue.
- **CRS R48216** (2024-10-08), https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48216/R48216.2.pdf
  - Swipe fees about $160bn in 2022 (credit and debit; based on NRF and Forbes estimates).
  - The merchant discount rate (MDR) is typically 1–3%.
  - Interchange goes to the cardholder's bank, but the card network sets it.
  - Network fee 0.1–0.2%.
  - Worked example: $100 sale, merchant receives $97.
- **CFPB, Credit Card Rewards issue spotlight** (2024-05), https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_credit-card-rewards_issue-spotlight_2024-05.pdf
  - Revolvers "pay 94 percent of total interest and fees, but gain less than 30 percent of rewards benefits".
  - Rewards over $40bn in 2022.
- **CFPB late-fee rule** (2024-03-05), https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/cfpb-bans-excessive-credit-card-late-fees-lowers-typical-fee-from-32-to-8/
  - Typical fee from $32 to $8; families would save "more than $10 billion" a year.
- **CFPB compliance page** (modified 2025-07-01), https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/consumer-cards-resources/credit-card-penalty-fees/
  - Rule vacated by court order on April 15, 2025.
- **Fed SHED 2025** (2026-05), https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-economic-well-being-of-us-households-in-2025-credit.htm
  - 82% of adults have a card.
  - 45% of owners carried a balance at least once in the past 12 months.
- **Visa 10-K, FY to 2026-09-30**, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm
  - Net revenue $45,903M; net income $23,504M; operating income $28,110M (margin ≈ 61.2%, computed).
  - Client incentives $18,317M.
- **Mastercard 10-K, FY2025**, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000013/ma-20251231.htm
  - Net revenue $32,791M; operating margin 57.6%; net income $14,968M.
- **American Express 10-K, FY2025**, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000000496226000080/axp-20251231.htm
  - Card Member rewards $18,409M; discount revenue $37,401M (worldwide).
