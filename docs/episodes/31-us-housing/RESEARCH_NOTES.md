## Verified
- **Harvard JCHS, State of the Nation's Housing 2026, Key Facts** (2026-06-17), https://www.jchs.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/interactive-item/files/Harvard_JCHS_State_Nations_Housing_2026_Key_Facts_0.pdf
  - Price-to-income: "falling slightly from the all-time high of 5.0 in 2022 to 4.7 in 2025. By comparison, this ratio was 4.1 in 2019 and averaged 3.2 throughout the 1990s."
  - "The monthly mortgage payment on the median-priced home is $2,420 ... payments on the median-priced home were $1,240 at the end of 2020 ... total monthly homeownership costs balloon to $3,120."
  - "The income needed to afford payments on the median-priced home was $120,800 at the end of 2025, up from $68,700 five years earlier. The income required exceeded $100,000 in 169 of the nation's 387 metros in 2025, up from 31 markets in 2020."
  - "just 23 percent of listings in March 2026 were at a price affordable to a household earning $75,000 or less, down from 49 percent of listings in March 2019."
  - "With half of all outstanding mortgages carrying rates below 4 percent as of late 2025 ..."; "Residential mobility fell to a record low of 11.2 percent in 2024."
  - "existing home sales were unchanged from 2024 at 4.1 million, stuck at a 30-year low that is two-thirds of the 6.1 million sales in 2021."
  - "various organizations estimate the shortage to be between 1.2 and 5.5 million units."
- **JCHS press release** (2026-06-17): "Home prices have increased 54 percent nationwide since 2020".
- **FRED MSPUS** (Census/HUD, median sales price of NEW houses sold, NSA): 2000Q3 $168,800; 2019Q3 $318,400; 2026Q3 $393,300.
- **FRED MORTGAGE30US** (Freddie Mac PMMS): low 2.65% (2021-01-07); 7.18% (2026-11-05).
- **FRED COMPUTNSA** (Census completions, our sums): 2000–09 ≈ 15.64M; 2010–19 ≈ 9.15M; 2011 = 585k (lowest year since the series starts in 1968); 2025 ≈ 1.49M.
- **Up for Growth, 2025 Housing Underproduction Report** (2025-11-24): shortfall "from 3.85 million homes in 2022 to 3.78 million homes in 2023."
- **NAHB, Government Regulation in the Price of a New Home: 2026** (2026-06-08): 26.4% of the final price; $131,734 on an average $499,500 new home. Trade-group estimate.
- **UC Berkeley Othering & Belonging Institute, Single-Family Zoning in Greater Los Angeles** (2022-03-02): "77.70 percent of residential land in Greater LA is exclusively reserved for single-family housing."
- **NAR press release** (2026-11-05): first-time share 22% (record low 21% the year before); "about half the historical norm of 40% seen before 2008"; median first-time buyer age 40 (all-time high).
