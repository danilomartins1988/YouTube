## Verified claims
- Opened 31 Jan 1990 at Pushkin Square; people queued from 4 a.m. (CBC).
- About 30,000 customers on day one, a McDonald's opening-day record (CBC). AP-1990: "30,000 meals on 27 cash registers". Staff had expected 10,000 (Ironside).
- 14 years of work and $50 million invested (CBC). Lobbying began in 1976 (Ironside).
- Joint venture of McDonald's Canada and the Moscow city soviet, signed 29 Apr 1987. City held 51%, McDonald's 49%. McDonald's rose to 80% in Feb 1996 (Ironside).
- Supply plant ("McComplex", 100,000 sq ft, Solntsevo; building started 1989, opened 1990) (Ironside). CBC: 14,000 buns an hour, 3,000 tons of potatoes a day, 10,000 patties an hour. Russet Burbank seed potatoes were imported and Soviet collective farmers trained (Ironside).
- Cost of a meal was half a day's average wages (CBC).
- By 2022: over 850 restaurants (10-Q2) and 62,000 employees (MCD-0516). McDonald's owned 84% of the locations (AP-Mar22). Russia and Ukraine together were 9% of revenue, about $2bn (AP-Mar22).
- 8 Mar 2022: temporary closure announced. 16 May 2022: exit announced; estimated charge $1.2–1.4bn. CEO: "we can no longer keep the Arches shining there."
- Sold to licensee Alexander Govor (MCD-0519), who keeps staff for 2 years. No price was disclosed.
- Charge booked: $1.2bn in Q2 and $1.3bn over six months (ER-Q2).
- Reopened as "Vkusno & tochka" on 12 Jun 2022 at Pushkin Square (AP-Jun22).
- Hook quote from the manager, Khamzat Khazbulatov: "Big Mac is perestroika." (Ironside)
