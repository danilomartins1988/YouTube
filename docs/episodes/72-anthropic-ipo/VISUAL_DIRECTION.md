# Visual direction: Anthropic's $1.5 Trillion IPO: The AI Gold Rush or the Next Bubble?

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/anthropics-1-5-trillion-ipo/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** strategy
2. **Secondary tones:** sitcom, thriller
3. **Colour palette:** cool greys and blues, one accent per rival (episode palette: bg #F5F6FA, accent #E8A317, risk #E5483B)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** clean, even, slight vignette
5. **Animation intensity:** measured, diagrammatic
6. **Character behaviour:** neutral: draws maps of rivals, decisions and consequences (demeanour `neutral`, at least 4 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** boardrooms, maps, company towers
8. **Charts and diagrams:** side-by-side comparisons, networks
9. **Transitions:** clean slides
10. **Music and sound:** C-major pad, light pulse; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "How it got so valuable": sitcom → strategy
    - inside `c4_critics` at "Circular financing": → thriller (the criticism gets its own spotlight (full weight, attributed))
    - inside `c6_bear` at "growth slows": → crisis (the dark road; storm only on that road)
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - No company logos: the company is shown by its ticker ANT and plain-text plaques; companies named in narration
    - NEUTRAL: Anthropic makes the AI model used in this production; bull case, bear case and circular-financing criticism with equal weight; filings and named press only; not investment advice
    - Every number on screen carries a claim pill (FACT / OUR CALC / REPORTED / OPINION / HYPOTHETICAL / ANALYSIS / STORY)
    - Public figures (Dario and Daniela Amodei, Andy Jassy, Sundar Pichai) only as respectful caricatures with name tags; private people as silhouettes; June is fictional
    - Day scenes, human characters, bespoke worlds (CartoonStage/antipo.tsx); USD only
