The Anthropic IPO, explained through one share. On November 19, 2026, June, a nurse from Ohio (a fictional character), gets one share of Anthropic at the $83 offer price. By lunch it is worth much more; by the close, a bit less. What did she, and Wall Street, actually buy? We follow that share through the record offering, the different valuations in the headlines, the revenue rocket, the cost of building intelligence and about $900 billion of computing promises, the money loop between Anthropic, Amazon and Google (the "circular financing" criticism and the other side), who got rich on paper and who can sell, and what a price of about twenty times its sales pace assumes on the bright, middle and dark roads. Our answer: neither a bubble by definition nor a sure thing; the price assumes the growth lasts a very long time.

Education and entertainment only. NOT investment advice and not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.

Key figures (the film rounds them)
- Offering: 980M Class A shares at $83 (range $70-$78) = $81.34B; 97.6% new shares from the company, 2.4% sold by the seven co-founders. Private placement: $26.0B at $83 (Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, TSMC). Total ≈$107.3B, a record (424B4; The National).
- Nov 19, 2026 (latest close when made): open $111.50, reported high $115.10, close $97.17, +17% (CNBC, Forbes, The National).
- Valuations: ≈13.22B shares outstanding × $97.17 ≈ $1.28T (our calc; reported ≈$1.27T). Fully diluted (adds options, stock awards, convertible notes): ≈$1.5T at $83 (CNBC), ≈$1.75-1.8T at the close (The National, CNBC). Proceeds ≠ market value; equity value ≠ enterprise value.
- Revenue: $41M (2023), $4.59B (2025), $33.24B (Jan-Sep 2026); run rate ≈$81B (Oct 31). Jan-Sep 2026: operating loss $1.26B; net loss $104.0B, incl. $94.3B non-cash fair-value changes; operating cash flow +$6.6B (424B4).
- Compute per $1 of revenue: ≈$1.60 (2025), ≈$0.57 (Jan-Sep 2026) (our calc, 424B4).
- Commitments ≈$900.9B to 2045, incl. ≥$110B Amazon, ≥≈$121.3B Google, ≥$47.2B Microsoft; debt $6.2B; cash and short-term investments ≈$132B on Sep 30 (424B4).
- Amazon: ≈$18B invested; carried at ≈$238.7B on Sep 30, before the IPO (our sum, Amazon 10-Q); 14.6% of capital stock after the offering; Google 13.4%. Cloud marketplaces: 44% of revenue, counted gross (424B4).
- Paper wealth: Series A ≈$0.26 per share (our calc); 1.77B employee options at $2.91 average; 400+ employees reported worth $100M+ (CNBC). Lock-up up to 180 days, 365 for co-founders and big holders (424B4).
- Value ÷ sales pace ≈21.6x (our calc). Hypothetical: at 25x earnings, ≈$1.75T implies ≈$70B a year of profit. Scenarios are illustrative, no probabilities.
- Criticism: Reuters described an "increasingly circular" relationship; a rival reportedly argued gross counting inflates revenue (Axios; both firms say they follow standard accounting); Tech Policy Press (opinion) says small investors bear the risk; the prospectus warns of possible "conflicts of interest".
