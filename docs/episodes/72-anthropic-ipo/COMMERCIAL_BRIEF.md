## 6. Payoff (specific)
What Wall Street bought: a share of one of the fastest-growing companies ever measured (revenue $41m in 2023 → $33.2bn in nine months of
2026; operating cash flow positive), attached to ≈$900bn of compute promises and to partners on every side of the table, priced at about
twenty times its sales pace (our calc). That is neither a bubble by definition nor a sure thing; the price assumes the growth lasts a very long
time. The steadiest winners may be the shovel sellers, some paid twice. "Importance and investment returns are two different things." Not
investment advice.
