## Narration in the Short

> What happens when the price of a metal doubles in a single morning, and the market where it trades decides that none of it happened? In March twenty twenty-two, it happened in London. The price of nickel, a metal used in stainless steel and batteries, shot past one hundred and one thousand three hundred and sixty-five dollars a tonne. The London Metal Exchange suspended trading. Then it cancelled every trade made that morning, worth about thirteen point four billion dollars. Some traders lost fortunes they thought they had made. Others were saved from ruin.
