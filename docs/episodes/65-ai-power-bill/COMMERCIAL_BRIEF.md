## 7. Payoff
Nationally, the forecast is "rising, not exploding" (≈ +13% over three years). In hot spots where data centers cluster and the grid runs
short, the capacity and wire slices can jump, and whether households pay depends on three dials: minimum-bill contracts, the speed of new
supply, and how shared lines are split. Insight: who pays for the AI boom's power is decided in auction rules and rate cases, not physics.
