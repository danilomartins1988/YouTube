## 3. Human question
How can Prime cost Amazon so much and still be one of its smartest deals?

## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
One hundred and two point seven billion dollars. That is what Amazon spent on shipping in a single year, twenty twenty-five. More than the entire economy of many countries produces. Now compare it with what Amazon earned from all its subscriptions, Prime fees, music, audiobooks and more. forty-nine point six billion dollars. By our calculation, the shipping bill alone was about twice as big. And Amazon says it plans to keep offering those shipping deals indefinitely. So how can a membership that seems to cost Amazon so much be one of the smartest deals in business?

## 7. Payoff (as rendered)
So what is the hidden economics of Amazon Prime? Prime is not a product that has to make money on its own. It is the glue. It keeps hundreds of millions of people shopping, watching and clicking in one place. Amazon may spend more on delivery than Prime fees bring in. But what it buys with that money is something far more valuable. Your habit.
