Amazon spent $102.7 billion on shipping in 2025, about twice what all its subscriptions brought in ($49.6 billion, including Prime fees, music, audiobooks and more; Amazon 10-K, ratio is our calculation). So how can a membership that seems to cost so much be one of the smartest deals in business? Four parts of the Prime machine: the fee ($79 in 2005, $139 today), the flywheel (shipping and video that Amazon itself calls "effective worldwide marketing tools"), the hidden profit engines (advertising at $68.6 billion and AWS, 57% of operating income), and the cancellation maze the FTC says Amazon called "Iliad", which ended in a $2.5 billion settlement.
