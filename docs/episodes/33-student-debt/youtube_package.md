Americans owe $1.65 trillion in student loans, more than they owe on all their credit cards (New York Fed, Q2 2026). When missed payments came back on credit reports in 2025, the share of student debt 90+ days late jumped from 0.5% to 7.7% in a single quarter, and reached 10.6% by mid-2026. Who is in this business? The colleges that set the price, the federal government that lends (and, by GAO's estimate, swung from an expected $114 billion profit to a $197 billion cost on loans made 1997-2021), the servicers paid to collect, and the borrowers who fall behind. Plus the new rules: SAVE vacated, Grad PLUS eliminated, new borrowing caps.
