# Visual direction: The Day Volkswagen Became the World's Most Valuable Company

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/the-day-volkswagen-became-the-most-valuable-company/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** thriller
2. **Secondary tones:** crisis, strategy, reflective
3. **Colour palette:** slate, ink blue, desaturated; gold for money, red for the lie (episode palette: bg #0D1220, accent #FFC21A, risk #FF4B3E)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** low-key, one pool of light on the evidence, heavy vignette, film grain
5. **Animation intensity:** slow, deliberate; moments of stillness before revelations
6. **Character behaviour:** serious: examines documents, connects evidence, points at gaps; never smiles or hops (demeanour `neutral`, at least 4 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** night offices, archives, vaults, courtrooms; no smiling sun, no clouds
8. **Charts and diagrams:** thin lines on dark screens, red highlights on inconsistencies
9. **Transitions:** slow cuts, scan lines, stamps
10. **Music and sound:** low A-minor pad with E-major tension, heartbeat pulse, 55 Hz drone; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "The squeeze": thriller → crisis
    - chapter "The hunter and the hunted": crisis → strategy
    - chapter "The lesson": strategy → reflective
    - inside `m2_options` at "very different": → crisis (the hidden position becomes visible)
    - inside `m5_court` at "acquitted": → reflective (the legal ending)
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - Financial district at night, trading floors, hedge-fund rooms; no smiling sun, no colourful city
    - The share grid (Porsche 74 / Lower Saxony 20 / < 6 for sale / ≈ 12 owed) is the visual key of the squeeze
    - Presenter neutral, no smiles in the squeeze
