Why day rates for October 2008: the euro fell sharply that autumn (2008 average 1.4726 vs 1.2476 on 28 Oct). For a
one-day comparison with ExxonMobil's dollar value, the day's rate is the honest one (at the annual average the figure
would be inflated to ≈ $436bn).
