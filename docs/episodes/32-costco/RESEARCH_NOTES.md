## Verified ($ millions; FY2026 / FY2025)
- Net sales 297,247 / 269,912; membership fees 5,907 / 5,323; total revenue 303,154 / 275,235; operating income 11,685 / 10,383; net income 9,226 / 8,099.
- Gross margin percentage 11.09% / 11.12% (10.92% FY2024). "these volumes and turnover enable us to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins ... than most other retailers."
- 939 warehouses (647 in the U.S. and Puerto Rico). Paid members 84.1M; total cardholders 150.4M; Executive members 42.3M, ≈ 75.2% of net sales.
- "Our member renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026."
- Fees since Sep 1, 2024: Gold Star $65; Executive $130 (from $120); max 2% reward $1,250; ~52M memberships affected.
- "We carry less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse ... significantly less than other broadline retailers."
- Kirkland Signature "now representing approximately $90B of annual revenue globally"; "typically offering 15-20% savings compared to the national brand equivalent" (2025 letter).
- Merchandise inventories 19,324; accounts payable 22,591 (Aug 30, 2026). "We often sell inventory before we are required to pay for it, even while taking advantage of early payment discounts."

## Derived (our calculation)
- Membership fees = 50.6% of operating income, 64.0% of net income, 1.9% of revenue (fees have costs: "equal to", not "come from").
- Average markup over cost ≈ 12.5% (gross margin / merchandise costs). Inventory turns ≈ 14×/yr (≈ 26 days); payables ≈ 29 days; AP = 117% of inventory.
