## Wording choices to check

- The ITU (1.7 million km) and TeleGeography (1.5 million km in service) differ; the film attributes 1.7 million to the ITU.
- "Pressed the members to drop it" summarises Reuters' "U.S. wins battle" and Light Reading's "US hardball".
- The repair sequence (find, lift, repair at sea) is a general description, labelled as such in the script.
- Map routes are schematic arcs between real places, stated on screen; company markers show countries, not sites.
