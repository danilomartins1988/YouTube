## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
Six point nine billion dollars. That is how much America's airports earned in a single year from parking and ground transportation. More than passenger airlines paid them to land their planes. Think about what that means. Before your plane even leaves the ground, the airport has already been paid. For your parking spot. Through a charge hidden in your ticket. In rent from the airline. And for the coffee you bought at the gate. So who actually owns these giant machines, and how do they make their money?

## 7. Payoff (as rendered)
So why do America's airports make money before you fly? Because an airport is not just a runway. It is a landlord, a parking operator, a shopping mall and a toll collector, all at once. And every one of those businesses gets paid long before your wheels leave the ground. So next time you land, look around. The garage, the rental counter, the coffee shop, the screens on the wall. Each one is part of how the airport pays its bills, long before anyone checks a boarding pass.
