In fiscal 2025, America's airports earned $6.9 billion from parking and ground transportation, more than the $5.5 billion that passenger airlines paid them in landing fees (FAA CATS). So how does an airport make money before you even board a plane? Four ways: the charges hidden in your ticket (a $4.50 passenger facility charge per segment, capped at $18 a round trip), the rent airlines pay for gates and terminal space, your car (parking, rental cars and ride-hail pickups), and the terminal itself, from coffee shops to advertising screens. Plus who actually owns most U.S. airports: states, counties, cities and public authorities, required by the FAA to be "as self-sustaining as possible."
