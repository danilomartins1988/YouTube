AI fact-check, 2026-11-09. Every claim was read on its source page before the script was written. Status: `source_located`

**Title note:** "$10 Billion" is the White House assessment of NotPetya's total damage (WIRED, confirmed by Tom Bossert), said
as an estimate in the film. "Froze" refers to Maersk's IT and terminals (17 of 76 terminals hit); the ships kept sailing,
and the film says so. "World's biggest shipping company": WIRED calls Maersk "the world's largest shipping conglomerate".

**Not used (could not be read on a primary page):** Maersk's own investor-relations page (403; the Q2 2017 statement is quoted
via SiliconANGLE); the DOJ press release (empty page; the indictment is cited via Lawfare and Infosecurity Magazine); the
exact date of the Merck settlement (the film says "early 2024", per McGuireWoods).
