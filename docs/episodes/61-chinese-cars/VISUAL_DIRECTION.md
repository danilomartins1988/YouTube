# Visual direction: Why Are Chinese Cars So Cheap — And So Advanced?

Generated by `tools/visual_direction.py` from the script, then reviewed. The YAML twin (`projects/why-are-chinese-cars-so-cheap/visual_direction.yaml`) is applied to the render: palette, scene tones, tone shifts, presenter demeanour and the music bed all follow it.

1. **Primary emotional tone:** sitcom
2. **Secondary tones:** strategy, trade
3. **Colour palette:** saturated flat primaries, bold outlines (episode palette: bg #F2F6FA, accent #FFB627, risk #E5484D)
4. **Lighting and contrast:** bright daylight, punchy
5. **Animation intensity:** constant motion, squash and stretch, whip pans
6. **Character behaviour:** warm: explains between gags (demeanour `neutral`, at least 5 presenter sequences, only where they explain)
7. **Background design:** bespoke retro-futurist sets
8. **Charts and diagrams:** bold bars, big numbers
9. **Transitions:** act bumpers, title card
10. **Music and sound:** major turnaround, 3 s chords, bouncy pulse; the bed changes with each chapter's tone and crossfades at turning points
11. **Key emotional turning points:**
    - chapter "The subsidy suspect": sitcom → strategy
    - chapter "The machine": strategy → trade
    - chapter "Follow the cash": trade → strategy
    - chapter "The price war": strategy → sitcom
    - inside `a4_unwind` at "Free cash flow": → strategy (the twist (a profitable company with deeply negative free cash flow) gets a held, cooler beat)
12. **Scene-specific requirements:**
    - Every car is a generic original design; no logos, badges or grilles of real brands; models named in text only
    - Every price shows its market and its taxes; every company figure shows its reporting period; our calculations labelled
    - Company-level vs automotive-segment results kept apart; grants vs buyer incentives vs equity financing vs supplier credit kept apart
    - No flags; countries named in text only; no stereotypes, accent jokes or mocking of any country, company or people
    - Real people only as respectful busts with a name label, doing only what the narration documents
    - Jokes never change a number; day scenes, many original human characters
