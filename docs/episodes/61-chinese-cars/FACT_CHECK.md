# Ep 61 fact check: Why Are Chinese Cars So Cheap — And So Advanced?

Every figure below was read on its primary filing or page on 2026-11-17 (notes: `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`; claims:
`projects/why-are-chinese-cars-so-cheap/sources.yaml`). Status `source_located` = the number is verbatim in the source;
`derived` = our calculation from sourced numbers (labelled on screen); `analysis` = explanation without figures; `estimate` = an
