## 6. Payoff (specific)
Canada has the rare full list: ~6 in 10 kWh from hydro (no. 3 in the world), free cooling much of the year, 34 critical minerals (no. 2 in
uranium), and the labs where deep learning grew up (a 2024 Nobel, the world's first national AI strategy). But the cheap power is
taken (Quebec's proposed data-center rate ≈ 2.5×, B.C. makes AI compete), mines take 16+ years, top talent and startups are pulled
south, and ~3 in 4 export dollars depend on a neighbor that tariffs. The answer: Canada could be a big winner, but only if it builds
(power lines, mines, data centers and companies that stay). The "crash" is real for tariffed industries; the AI boom bets on a different
Canada.
