> One point six billion dollars. That is how much customer money was missing when the brokerage firm MF Global collapsed in October twenty eleven, according to the trustee who took over the firm. This was not the firm's own money. It belonged to its customers. Among them were farm cooperatives representing up to thirty-five thousand farmers, who used the firm to protect themselves against swings in crop prices. So how does customer money simply vanish from a regulated firm? And did those farmers ever get it back? By the end of this video, you will know what a futures broker does with your money, the bet that sank MF Global, what happened in its last week, and how long it took to find the money.
