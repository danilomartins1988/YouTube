Key figures (the film rounds them; 424B4 unless noted)
- Share classes: A (1 vote, trades as ANT); N (same money, no vote, mostly partners); T (one share, Long-Term Benefit Trust, elects most of the board); F (one share, co-founders' LLC, 50.1% of votes); E (employees, limited votes). A controlled company under Nasdaq rules.
- Shares: 13.22B outstanding (7.58B A + 5.64B N); 980M sold in the IPO = 7.4% (our calc). Not counted: 1.77B options, 1.03B unvested RSUs, Amazon notes above a cap (convert quarterly), plan reserves, possible Public Benefit Issuances (up to 2% each +50% price milestone). Fully diluted ≈ 17.6-18B (our calc; press).
- Price to trailing sales, Nov 19 closes (our calc): ANT ≈ 36x ($1.28T ÷ $35.3B; ≈ 50x fully diluted; ≈ 16x the $81B Oct 31 run rate); Nvidia ≈ 9.7x; Microsoft ≈ 10.7x; Alphabet ≈ 7.1x; Meta ≈ 6.3x. Market value, not EV: ≈ $132B cash and investments (Sep 30) + ≈ $105B net proceeds; debt $6.2B.
- Lock-up steps (up to): Nov 19 315M; Dec 15 303M; Jan 5 303M; Jan 19 353M; Feb 2 353M; after the FY2026 results 1.7B, then 5 × 480M; May 17, 2027 (or earlier, after the Jan-Mar results) 3.0B; more after the Q1/Q2 2027 results; Nov 18, 2027 (or earlier) 1.9B for co-founders and holders above 0.5%. The $26B placement is not locked up but restricted (Rule 144). Unlocked ≠ sold.
- First report card: revenue growth; compute cost per $1 of revenue (≈ $1.60 in 2025, ≈ $0.57 in Jan-Sep 2026, our calc); operating cash flow (+$6.6B, Jan-Sep 2026); commitments ≈ $900.9B to 2045 (≈ $70.7B due within 12 months of Sep 30, 2026). Results date not announced as of Nov 20, 2026.
- Dependence: Amazon 14.6%, Google 13.4% of shares; both supply compute and resell Claude (marketplaces: 44% of Jan-Sep 2026 revenue); chips from AWS, Google, Nvidia, AMD; no customer above 10% in Jan-Sep 2026 (two at 12% in 2025). The prospectus warns of possible "conflicts of interest".
- Precedents, not predictions: Facebook (IPO $38) hit a new low as its first lock-up ended Aug 16, 2012 (AP), closed $17.73 on Sep 4, 2012, $26.25 a year after its IPO. Arm (IPO $51) closed $47.87 on Oct 20, 2023, then +48% on Feb 8, 2024 after earnings (Bloomberg). Closes: Yahoo Finance.
