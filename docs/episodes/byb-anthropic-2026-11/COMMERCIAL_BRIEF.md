## 7. Payoff
A buyer of ANT gets one vote per Class A share in a company whose votes and board majority are already held by the founders' share and an
independent trust; pays about 36 times trailing sales (≈16x the latest run rate), a bet that fast growth lasts; and faces a lock-up staircase
whose biggest step (up to 3.0bn shares) comes by May 17, 2027. The first annual report tells the most: growth, compute cost per dollar,
cash flow and the commitment schedule. Precedents show the first year can swing either way around exactly those dates.
