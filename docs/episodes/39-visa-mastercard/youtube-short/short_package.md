> Three hundred and fifty-five billion. That is how many payments carried Visa's name in a single year. Close to one billion every single day. And yet Visa does not lend you money. So how did two clubs of banks become the gate that almost every card payment in America has to pass through? By the end of this video, you will know how that gate was built, why it is so hard to go around it, and why the courts and Congress keep trying to pry it open.
