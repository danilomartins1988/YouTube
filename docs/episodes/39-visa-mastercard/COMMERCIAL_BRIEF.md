## 3. Human question
How did two card networks end up taking a cut of almost every purchase?

## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
Three hundred and fifty-five billion. That is how many payments carried Visa's name in a single year. Close to one billion every single day. And yet Visa does not lend you money. Neither does Mastercard. They did not even start as companies. They started as clubs, owned by the banks that used them. So how did two clubs of banks become the gate that almost every card payment in America has to pass through?

## 7. Payoff (as rendered)
So how did Visa and Mastercard become gatekeepers? Not by lending money, and not by owning stores. They became the meeting point. First as clubs that let thousands of banks act like one. Then as companies sitting on a loop that gets stronger every time someone opens a card or a store accepts one. That is the real business. Not the plastic, but the place where everyone has to meet.
