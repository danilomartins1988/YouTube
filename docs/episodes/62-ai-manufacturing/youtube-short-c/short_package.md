> So, could AI make American factories cheaper than China's? For some products, yes. The likeliest are heavy or bulky goods that are costly to ship, products built from many repeated precision steps, and things a country can't risk running out of, like chips, batteries and medical supplies. Economists Daron Acemoglu and Pascual Restrepo found that in American local job markets, the more robots arrived, the fewer people were employed and the lower wages went. The new jobs are different.
