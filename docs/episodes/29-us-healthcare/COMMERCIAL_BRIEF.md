## 3. Human question
Why do Americans pay more for healthcare but don't get more of it?

## 4. First 30 seconds (as rendered)
Twenty-eight thousand dollars. That is what health insurance for the average American family with a job-based plan now costs in a single year. And here is the strange part. Americans see a doctor about half as often as people in other rich countries. They spend fewer days in hospital. Yet they pay far more for health care, and they live shorter lives. So if Americans are not getting more care, where is all that money going?

## 7. Payoff (as rendered)
So why do Americans pay so much for health care? Not because they go to the doctor more. They go less. They pay more for each visit, each scan, each pill, and each claim. Health care in America is not expensive because of how much care people get. It is expensive because of what each piece of care costs.
