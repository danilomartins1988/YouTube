## Things deliberately NOT claimed
- No claim that any named retailer sells doorbusters below cost (loss-leader logic is described generically).
- No credit-card interchange rate (the Fed data cover debit/prepaid only).
- No dollar cost of processing returns (no solid public figure found).
- The Which? study is not presented as U.S. evidence.
- Adobe's 2025 electronics peak: Adobe's Sep 2026 forecast quotes 30.1% for 2025 while its Jan 2026 final release says 30.9%; we use the final release and round to "about thirty-one percent".
