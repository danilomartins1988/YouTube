## Narration in the Short

> Some deals are built for the day itself. Consumer Reports has described low-priced TVs made specifically for Black Friday, often sold by a single retailer. The industry calls them derivatives. They're similar to regular models, but with different model numbers and often fewer features, like fewer HDMI inputs. To be fair, one derivative that Consumer Reports tested cost about one hundred and seventy dollars less than the model it was based on, and performed just as well. The catch is comparison. If only one store sells that model number, you can't easily check the price anywhere else.
