A Caribbean island of about 16,000 people never built an AI. But it owns two letters: .ai. In the first seven months of 2026 those letters brought in $80 million, 47.5% of all the money Anguilla's government collected, more than all the taxes paid by its businesses.

In this animated documentary:
- How an island with no tech industry ended up with the most wanted letters on the internet
- How the money machine works: $140 for two years, renewals, auctions
- The boom: from 144,000 names in 2022 to more than one million in 2026
- What the money pays for: the airport, roads, health care, tax relief
- Can it last? The lessons of .tv and .io
