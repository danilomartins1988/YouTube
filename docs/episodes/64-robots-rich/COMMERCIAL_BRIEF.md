## 3. Human question
Dana, a (fictional) warehouse shift lead in Ohio, watches robots arrive while the warehouse earns more than ever: if the robots do the work, who gets the money, and does she own any of it?

## 4. First 30 seconds

the clue: workers' share of business output is the lowest on record (BLS); the flip: "will I keep my job?" → "do I own any of the machines?".

## 7. Payoff
On the evidence so far, the gains go mostly to the owners of the machines, a group where the richest tenth hold nearly the whole pile
of stock. But the split is not fixed: history shows pay can catch up after a delay, new jobs keep appearing, and more than half of
families already own some stock. Close: the old question "what do you do for a living?" gains a second part: "what do you own?".
