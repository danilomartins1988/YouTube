Who gets rich from AI when robots do the work? Follow Dana, a warehouse shift lead in Ohio (a fictional character), as robots arrive and her warehouse earns more than ever. We follow one $100 bill of American income to see how much goes to workers and how much to owners, line up every U.S. household to see who actually owns the stock market, and look at the AI building boom that is creating the biggest machines in history. Then we separate what researchers have measured about AI, jobs and wages from what they only forecast, revisit Britain's "Engels' pause", and give the counterarguments a fair hearing. This film is education, not investment advice. Nothing in it recommends buying anything.

Key figures and sources (read Nov 18, 2026; "our calc." = our arithmetic):
- Workers' share of nonfarm business output: BLS labor share index 93.7 in Q3 2026 (2017=100), lowest since 1947; peak 117.1 in 1960 (BLS via FRED, PRS85006173; preliminary).
- Per $100 of U.S. gross domestic income: employee pay $58.40 (1970) vs $50.90 (2025); corporate profits after tax $4.50 vs $9.30 (BEA via FRED). The 7.5-point gap x $30.9T GDI is about $2.3T a year (our calc.; illustrative).
- Household stocks and stock funds, Q2 2026: richest 10% hold 88.1%, bottom 50% hold 0.6%; 401(k)-type accounts: top 10% 55.5%, bottom 50% 4.8% (Federal Reserve, Distributional Financial Accounts).
- 56% of families own stock directly or indirectly; 31% of the lower-income half (Federal Reserve, Survey of Consumer Finances 2025).
- Capital spending, 12 months to Sep 30, 2026: Amazon $210.7B, Alphabet $159.8B, Microsoft $135.9B, Meta $108.0B, about $614B combined (company filings; our sum). Amazon free cash flow: an outflow of $42.4B.
- Nvidia, quarter ended Oct 25, 2026: revenue $115.8B, net income $65.3B, about 56% (8-K; our calc.).
- AI assistant for 5,179 support agents: +14% issues resolved per hour, +34% for novices (Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, NBER w31161).
- Denmark: no measurable effect of AI chatbots on earnings or hours (Humlum & Vestergaard, NBER w33777).
- Workers aged 22-25 in AI-exposed jobs: employment 19% below the path of less-exposed peers; descriptive, not causal (Brynjolfsson, Chandar & Chen, Stanford Digital Economy Lab, Aug 2026).
- Robots 1990-2007: one more robot per 1,000 workers cut the employment ratio by 0.18-0.34 points and wages by 0.25-0.5% (Acemoglu & Restrepo, NBER w23285).
- Forecast: AI adds no more than 0.66% to total factor productivity over 10 years (Acemoglu, NBER w32487).
- Engels' pause: Allen, R. C. (2009), Explorations in Economic History 46(4).
- New work: Autor, Chin, Salomons & Seegmiller, NBER w30389. Superstar firms: Autor et al., NBER w23396.
- Median weekly pay, full-time, 25+, Q3 2026: bachelor's degree or higher $1,752, high school $993 (BLS via FRED).
- Employee stock ownership plans: 15.1 million participants (NCEO, 2023 plan data).
