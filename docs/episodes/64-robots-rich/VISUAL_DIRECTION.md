- No logos: data centers and chips are generic; company names appear only as text plaques.
- Pills (`rrtag`): FACT / FINDING / FORECAST / DESCRIPTIVE / OUR MATH / MECHANISM / EDUCATION, NOT ADVICE on every numeric scene.
- Story over stats: one big number at a time, charts ≤3 labels, exact figures in small source lines only.
- Presenter boards (5): p04 the $100 bill, p08 labor-share line, p19 the AI money loop, p30 Engels' pause, p39 doors to owning.
