## Methodology notes

- **Alphabet:** Class B is unlisted, so we valued it at the GOOGL (Class A) price. Meta's unlisted Class B is valued at the META price. SpaceX's Class B is valued at the SPCX price. Palantir's Class B and Class F are valued at the PLTR price.
- **Nvidia:** the cover page gives only a rounded "24.1 billion". Rounding error is about ±$8B, which has no effect on the rank.
- **Stale share dates:** Nvidia, Broadcom and Walmart still use August-dated counts because their next 10-Q filings have not yet been made. Buybacks since then would lower the true current figure slightly.
- **Rank 10 versus 11:** Walmart ($996B) leads SK hynix's ADS-implied $945B (which is excluded anyway) and AMD's $845B by a clear margin.
