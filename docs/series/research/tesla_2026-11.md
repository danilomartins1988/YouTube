**Reverse DCF (illustration, our calculation). The method breaks here.** TTM FCF of $3.3B is about 0.2% of the market cap, so the result is dominated by the assumed growth path and swings widely with small input changes. We show it only to make that point, then fall back to P/S and P/E (above).
Formula: `MarketCap = Σ_{t=1..10} FCF0·(1+g)^t/(1+r)^t + [FCF0·(1+g)^10·(1+gT)/(r−gT)]/(1+r)^10`. We solve for g.
Inputs: market cap $1,501.2B. FCF0 $3.303B. r = 8% and 10%. gT = 3%. 10 years. Net cash (about $36B) is ignored.

| Case | r = 8% | r = 10% |
|---|---|---|
| FCF0 = TTM $3.30B | g ≈ **44.7%/yr** (FCF year 10 ≈ $133B) | g ≈ **51.8%/yr** (FCF year 10 ≈ $214B) |
| FCF0 = FY2025 $6.22B | g ≈ 35.5%/yr | g ≈ 42.0%/yr |
| Market cap on diluted shares ($1.345T), TTM FCF | g ≈ 43.1%/yr | g ≈ 50.0%/yr |
| Zero growth for 10 years, then 3% | value ≈ $54B | value ≈ $39B |

Script reading: "Today's free cash flow can't explain the price. Our illustration needs FCF to grow about 45-52% a year for a decade, ending above $130-210B a year. Compare that with $3.3B in the last twelve months. The simpler picture is 14.5 times sales and about 466 times profit."

## 6. What has to happen (only as the company states it, tied to numbers)

1. **Autonomy turns into fleet revenue.** "We started providing Robotaxi rides using Cybercabs on September 3 in Austin." The fleet reached "nearly two million cumulative unsupervised miles … as of the end of the quarter", and unsupervised miles "grew eightfold quarter over quarter". FSD subscriptions were 1.74M (vs 1.04M a year earlier). The milestones in Musk's award are 10M active FSD subscriptions and 1M Robotaxis in commercial operation (10-Q). The company does not report Robotaxi revenue separately.
2. **Energy storage keeps growing at about 30% gross margin.** The deck reports the "highest-ever order rate and backlog". Megafactory Texas "began production, with planned capacity of 50 GWh". TTM energy GP was $3.6B on $12.3B revenue (Computed). The 10-Q warns: "The current tariff regime will have a relatively larger impact on our energy generation and storage business".
3. **Optimus becomes a product.** "Optimus production began in Fremont on our high-volume lines … we have proven that the current manufacturing process for Optimus is suitable for a commercial product." The award milestone is "1 million bots delivered". There are no revenue figures.
4. **Car volume recovers with batteries as the bottleneck.** "battery availability continued to be the limiting factor in increasing auto volumes"; "largest backlog for our passenger vehicles in four years". Deliveries were 1.64M in 2025 vs 1.79M in 2024. The award's first operational milestone is 20M cumulative vehicles (10.2M to date).

**Musk pay package (relevant, Fetched 10-Q).** 12 tranches of 35,311,992 shares each (423.7M in total). Each tranche needs a market-cap milestone from **$2.0T to $8.5T** plus operational milestones (for example, $50B to $400B Adjusted EBITDA; TTM is $15.0B). **Today's $1.50T is below the first $2.0T tranche.** Unrecognized expense for the probable milestone is $9.74B, to be recognized over 8.9 years. Under the 2025 award, vested shares are "reduced by an offset amount of $334.09 per share". The 2018-award restricted shares require service through 2028-01-19.

## 7. What could go wrong (Fetched: 10-K FY2025 and 10-Q)

- **Key person.** "We are highly dependent on the services of Elon Musk … he does not devote his full time and attention to Tesla. For example: Mr. Musk also currently holds management positions at Space Exploration Technologies Corp., X.AI Holdings Corp. ('xAI'), Neuralink Corp. and The Boring Company". xAI was merged into SpaceX on 2026-02-02 (DEF 14A). SpaceX is now itself a listed top-10 Nasdaq company.
- **Share pledges.** "If Mr. Musk were to choose to partially secure any loans with pledges of Tesla common stock … Mr. Musk may be forced … to sell shares".
- **Award incentives.** "If the 2025 CEO Performance Award inadvertently directs our focus and resources toward specific technologies that are misaligned with market preferences … we may miss opportunities".
- **Demand and autonomy.** "Our future growth and success are dependent upon demand for our electric vehicles and adoption of autonomous driving solutions."
- **Competition.** "We face strong competition for our products and services from a growing list of established and new competitors."
- **Incentives and credits.** "recent governmental and regulatory actions have repealed and/or restricted consumer, manufacturing and charging infrastructure tax credits, and certain regulatory credit programs tied to our products." This includes the OBBBA (July 2025).
- **Litigation with $ amounts:**
  - *Benavides v. Tesla* (Autopilot fatality): "The jury awarded $129 million in total compensatory damages, finding the driver 67% at fault and the Company 33% at fault. The jury also awarded $200 million in punitive damages." The case is on appeal to the 11th Circuit; Tesla "has recorded an immaterial accrual".
  - A securities class action on Autopilot, FSD and Robotaxi claims (W.D. Tex., filed 2025-08-04); a motion to dismiss is fully briefed.
  - The FSD consumer class certification was vacated by the 9th Circuit on 2026-10-16.
  - A California CRD race-discrimination bench trial began 2026-09-21. EEOC case.
  - "We are unable to reasonably estimate the possible loss or range of loss" (most matters).
- **Regulators (NHTSA, NTSB, SEC, DOJ).** "The ongoing requests for information include topics such as … Advanced Driver-Assistance Systems and Robotaxi". **No $ amounts disclosed.** "To our knowledge no government agency in any ongoing investigation has concluded that any wrongdoing occurred."
- **Margin squeeze.** TTM operating margin was 3.3%. Opex rose to $4.78B in Q3 (vs $3.43B a year earlier), driven by "SI and other R&D projects, SBC (including 2025 CEO Performance Award)".

## 10. Flags

- **Share-count ambiguity (biggest flag).** 3.953B cover shares include about 710M restricted or unearned CEO shares. Our market cap is $1.50T; on diluted weighted shares it is $1.345T. State the method on screen.
- **"Super Intelligence (SI)".** In 2026 filings Tesla relabeled "AI" as "SI"; the 10-K said "AI initiatives" and the 10-Q says "SI initiatives". Quote the company's wording and do not explain it as a new product.
- **The reverse DCF is not meaningful** at a 0.22% FCF yield. Say so explicitly and do not present 45-52% as a forecast.
- **Net income includes non-operating marks** (SpaceX stake, bitcoin). TTM P/E is very sensitive to these.
- **Segments.** Tesla reports only segment gross profit. Do not show "segment operating margins". The "services" segment is folded into automotive.
- **The Robotaxi and Optimus claims are the company's.** There is no separately disclosed revenue. Attribute every statement ("Tesla says").
- **Analyst consensus is unverified.** Do not show it.
- **Wording.** Do not say "overvalued", "fair value", "buy" or "sell". The market-cap milestones in the pay package are compensation terms, not company price targets. Do not frame them as forecasts.
