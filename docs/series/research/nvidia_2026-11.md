## Q3 FY2027 (8-K)
- Revenue $115,844M (+20% q/q, +103% y/y; Q3 FY26 $57,006M). Data Center $107.6bn (+21% q/q, +110% y/y): Hyperscale
  $60.1bn (+71% y/y), AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise $47.5bn (+195%). Edge Computing $8.2bn (+42%).
  Segments: Compute & Networking $106.0bn, Graphics $9.8bn.
- Gross margin GAAP 74.8% / non-GAAP 74.9% (Q2 75.0%); "decreased slightly sequentially, reflecting the initial Rubin ramp".
- Opex GAAP $9,156M; operating income $77,520M; other income $1,497M (net equity gains $1.8bn: +$2.8bn public, −$1.0bn
  private incl. $1.8bn impairment); tax rate 17.4%; net income $65,287M; diluted EPS $2.70 GAAP / $2.68 non-GAAP.
  Non-GAAP no longer excludes stock-based compensation (since Q1 FY2027).
- Cash flow: OCF $48,253M; capex (PP&E + intangibles) $4,422M; principal payments $54M; FCF $43,777M (company).
  Repurchases $22,122M; dividends $6,026M; returned $28.1bn. Authorization +$150bn (Sep 2026); $227.3bn left, "which we
  plan to utilize through fiscal 2028". Next dividend $0.25 (payable Dec 23, 2026; record Dec 2). Q4 FY26 release:
  $0.01 dividend paid Apr 1, 2026.
- Balance sheet: cash + marketable debt $53.5bn; marketable equity $44.2bn; non-marketable securities $65.5bn; AR
  $81.0bn (DSO 64 vs 60, "extended payment terms on large, multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade
  customers"); inventory $39.7bn (from $31.6bn); long-term debt $32.4bn (repaid $1.0bn notes in Q3).
- Customer concentration (SUPP): Q3 three direct customers 14%, 11%, 11%; nine months four at 13%, 12%, 12%, 11%; two
  direct customers 18% and 17% of AR. Names not disclosed.
- Geography by customer HQ (SUPP): US $72,899M; Taiwan $30,295M; China incl. HK $11,050M; other $1,600M. CFO: Hopper
  shipments to China $2.4bn in Q3.
- Commitments (CFO): supply $390bn (from $279bn, mainly multi-year memory); total $518bn; neocloud agreements up to $32bn
  (contingent on the neoclouds' financing; possible revenue share); guarantees max $118.0bn incl. SB Energy credit
  support up to $105bn (≈ 4.25 GW, Ohio, 20-year leases to OpenAI; first phase expected FY2029); "To date, we have not
  made any payments related to our guarantees."
- Outlook Q4 FY2027 (includes an additional week): revenue $130.0bn ± 3%, no Data Center compute revenue from China
  assumed; GM 71.0-72.0%; opex GAAP ≈ $11.0bn; FY tax rate 16-18%.
- Forward-looking statements list the leadership transition / CEO search and "the ongoing investigation by an independent
  committee of NVIDIA's board of directors" among risks. No details in any filing read.

## CEO transition (8-K, Nov 12)
Board accepted Huang's resignation as President, CEO and director on Nov 11, 2026, effective immediately. Colette Kress
interim CEO (remains EVP and CFO); search with internal and external candidates; Harvey C. Jones appointed Chair (first
ever). No reason stated. Press-reported allegations covered in Ep 52/53 are NOT used in this episode (series rule:
company filings only; they remain unresolved allegations). Secondary YouTube titles (Nov 16-17) mention a "return of
Jensen Huang": nothing in EDGAR or the Q3 release supports it; not used.
