## 8. Governance (424B4)
- Public benefit corporation; specific public benefit purpose: "the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term
  benefit of humanity"; the board balances stockholders' pecuniary interests, the best interests of those materially affected, and the purpose.
- LTBT: five financially disinterested voting trustees; Class T elects a majority of the board; risk factor: "the LTBT may have interests that
  differ from yours, and it may exercise its power in a way with which you disagree or in a way that may be adverse to your interests."
- Co-founders each pledged 80% of personal equity to philanthropy (not used in narration).
