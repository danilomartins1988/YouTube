**Reverse DCF (illustration, our calculation).**
Formula: `MarketCap = Σ_{t=1..10} FCF0·(1+g)^t/(1+r)^t + [FCF0·(1+g)^10·(1+gT)/(r−gT)]/(1+r)^10`. We solve for g.
Inputs: market cap $1,639.5B (2026-11-10). FCF0 = TTM FCF $25.13B. r = 8% and 10%. gT = 3%. 10 years. Equity value is set equal to market cap; net debt is about zero at 9/30 (cash $82.4B vs debt $84.0B), so it is ignored.

| Case | r = 8% | r = 10% |
|---|---|---|
| FCF0 = TTM $25.13B | **g ≈ 17.7%/yr** (FCF year 10 ≈ $128B) | **g ≈ 23.2%/yr** (FCF year 10 ≈ $202B) |
| Sensitivity: FCF0 = FY2025 $43.6B | g ≈ 10.6%/yr | g ≈ 15.7%/yr |
| Zero growth for 10 years, then 3% | value ≈ $408B | value ≈ $297B |

Script reading: "At $1.64T, the price works if Meta's free cash flow grows roughly 18-23% a year for ten years from today's $25B, which is depressed by capex. Measured from 2025's $43.6B instead, the required growth is roughly 11-16%." Show inputs and date on screen.

## 6. What has to happen (tied to filing numbers)

1. **Ad pricing and volume keep compounding.** Q3 ad revenue +26% came from impressions +10% and price per ad +14%. 9M price per ad +13% is "mostly due to ongoing improvements to our ad performance from our ad targeting and measurement tools" (10-Q). The reverse DCF needs this mid-teens to 20%-plus FCF growth to persist.
2. **Capex has to turn back into free cash flow.** TTM capex was $108B (44.7% of revenue), Q3 FCF was −$2.12B, and Q4 implies $49-59B of capex. Meta: "MSL investments … will take longer to generate returns than those in our core business." The $454.5B of commitments ($121.4B due in 2027) make this the central condition.
3. **FoA margin holds once legal charges are excluded.** FoA margin was 51.6% in FY2025 and about 42% in Q3 2026 excluding the charge (Computed). Depreciation rose to $7.15B in Q3 from $4.96B a year earlier (Fetched, cash flow statement).
4. **Reality Labs losses shrink as guided:** $19.2B (2025) → $18-19B (2026 guide) → about $15B (2027 outlook).

## 7. What could go wrong (Fetched: 10-Q unless noted)

- **New Mexico Attorney General.**
  - "On October 21, 2026, the court issued an order imposing a civil penalty in the amount of $25 billion, assigning 15% post-judgment interest … We have accrued an amount for loss contingencies related to this matter and intend to appeal. We may be required to post a bond".
  - Earlier: a $375M jury civil penalty (Mar 2026) and about $567M in public-nuisance abatement (Aug 2026, stayed).
  - The amount accrued is **not disclosed**.
- **Settlement with the state attorneys general.** "payments of up to approximately $18.59 billion". "Approximately $12.7 billion … payable … in annual installments over a 10-year period". About $5.3B is contingent and not accrued; it becomes payable only if peers such as YouTube and TikTok adopt similar protections. Legal-related accruals were $19.88B at 9/30 ($11,577M current + $8,304M non-current), up from $7.3B at 12/31/2025.
- **Other youth cases.** Personal-injury, school-district (next bellwether February 2027) and mass-arbitration cases continue: "counsel for over two hundred thousand individual claimants have sent mass arbitration demands". Also: "the damages … plaintiffs have indicated they could seek … have at times in certain cases amounted to more than a trillion dollars" (claimed by plaintiffs, not an estimate).
- **District of Columbia.** The DC Attorney General is "seeking up to $1.7 billion in civil penalties".
- **EU.**
  - DMA "subscription for no ads": "imposed a fine of EUR €200 million" (April 2025, appealed). "further modifications to our model may be imposed … which could result in … a significant impact to our European business and revenue." Europe was 23% of TTM revenue (Computed).
  - Facebook Marketplace (Art. 102): "a fine of approximately EUR €798 million" (appealed).
  - WhatsApp Business API: an interim measure requires free access for general-purpose AI providers (June 2026).
  - Digital Services Act: preliminary findings on minors (April and July 2026).
  - Spain: AMI v. Meta, damages of about EUR €542 million (appealed); the UTECA judgment is expected in H1 2027. France and Italy cases follow.
- **FTC.** Meta won the monopolization case (2025-11-18); "On January 20, 2026, the FTC filed a notice of appeal." The FTC also has a separate proceeding to modify the 2020 privacy consent order.
- **AI spending risk factor:** "If our investments are not successful longer-term, our business and financial performance could be harmed."
- **Infrastructure risk factor:** "our industry has experienced shifting public sentiment toward data center development, as well as related legislative developments".
- **Key person and control:**
  - "We currently depend on the continued services and performance of our key personnel, including Mark Zuckerberg. Mr. Zuckerberg and certain other members of management participate in various high-risk activities, such as combat sports, extreme sports, and recreational aviation".
  - "the issuance of the Class C common stock … could have the effect of prolonging the duration of Mr. Zuckerberg's ability to exercise voting rights with respect to a majority of the voting power".
- **Signal and platform dependence:** Meta's ads rely on "data signals … that we do not control" (Apple and Google OS changes).

- **AI models and agents.** Zuckerberg: "launching Muse and Muse Wonder, our first frontier model". The 10-Q: "we have launched Muse, an agentic AI, which we intend to scale over time." The CFO says MSL creates "significant new revenue opportunities" (claim, not sized).

## 10. Flags

- **Next-edition share count.** After the Class C dividend (charter effective 2027-01-19), the share count will roughly double and the per-share price will roughly halve. The Feb 2027 refresh must sum Classes A + B + C and must not compare per-share prices across the split without adjusting.
- **Market cap excludes** the METAM preferred (about $12.8B) and the 46M forward-sale shares (not yet issued). Our $1.64T will differ from vendor figures.
- **"FCF" is Meta's non-GAAP definition** and subtracts finance-lease principal. Say "free cash flow as Meta defines it" or "our calc".
- **TTM net income is distorted** by the +$8.03B tax benefit and −$12.29B legal charges. Do not call P/E "normal".
- **The NM $25B accrual amount is not disclosed.** Do not claim it is fully reserved or fully unreserved.
- **The Q3 FoA margin excluding the charge (about 42%)** assumes the whole $9.89B sits in FoA. The 10-Q puts legal costs in FoA but does not give the exact segment split.
- **Analyst consensus is unverified.** Do not show it.
- **The $56B capital raise figure is Computed.** The forward-sale proceeds ($28.7B) are not yet received, and the price adjusts daily.
- **Wording.** Do not say "Meta is cheap/expensive", "fair value", "buy" or "sell". Say "the price assumes …". The plaintiffs' "more than a trillion dollars" is a claim by plaintiffs, not an exposure estimate, so attribute it.
