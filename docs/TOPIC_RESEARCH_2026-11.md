# Topic research: candidate episodes, November 2026

Prepared 2026-11-10 for The Hidden Economics: about 11-minute animated explainers in American English, mainly for U.S. viewers.

**Ground rules applied**
- Nothing below overlaps the 40 delivered or queued episodes, or the excluded Japan property bubble.
- Near-duplicates are flagged where a topic touches an existing episode.
- Every figure must come from primary sources. A hook marked **(checked)** was read on the primary page or primary dataset on 2026-11-10. Everything marked **(to verify)** came from search snippets or secondary reporting and must be read on the named primary source before it goes into a script.

**How the scores work**
- **Audience (1-5)** combines four things:
  - how relatable the topic is to U.S. viewers;
  - news or search pull in 2025-26;
  - Reddit and personal-finance question volume;
  - view counts on comparable videos.
- **Originality (1-5)** is 5 when no large explainer channel has made the angle, and 1 when it has been done to death recently.
- Coverage was checked with live YouTube searches on 2026-11-10. The view counts and ages quoted below are what those searches returned that day.
- **Ranking:** by audience plus originality; ties go to the higher audience score.

## Top 10 to make next

These are chosen for a mix of the five content buckets, not strictly by rank.

1. **Who Actually Gets the $166 Billion Tariff Refund?** *(everyday money and policy)* Americans paid the tariffs, but the refunds go to importers. Follow the money from the checkout to CBP and back to Walmart.
2. **Why America Stopped Making the Penny** *(everyday money)* A coin that cost 3.69¢ to make, a nickel that is even worse, and what rounding at the register really costs you.
3. **America's $1 Trillion of Gold Is Booked at $11 Billion** *(hidden money, policy)* A 1973 price tag of $42.22, a vault nobody audits like a bank, and the idea of revaluing it to pay down debt.
4. **One Factory Makes 60% of America's IV Bags** *(hidden supply chain)* One hurricane, one town in North Carolina, and the hospital surgeries that were cancelled across the country.
5. **How Prediction Markets Make Money Off Your Hunches** *(how X makes money)* There is no house edge, only fees. Sports betting through a federal derivatives loophole, and three appeals courts that disagree.
6. **Why Banks Still Make Billions From Overdrafts** *(everyday money)* Revenue halved from $11.96bn to $5.83bn, the CFPB rule died, and a few banks still collect most of it.
7. **Why the Post Office Loses $9 Billion a Year** *(how X makes money, or doesn't)* A 2006 law, falling letter volume, and a universal-service promise nobody wants to price.
8. **Alaska: The State That Pays You to Live There** *(economics of places)* America's only universal cash dividend, and why politicians keep shrinking it.
9. **College Sports Just Got a $20.5 Million Payroll** *(how X makes money)* A $2.8bn settlement made college athletes employees in all but name. Who pays, and which sports get cut.
10. **MF Global: The $1.6 Billion That Vanished** *(dramatic event)* A Wall Street firm run by a former senator and governor, a bet on European debt, and farmers' money that disappeared for months.

## Risks

**Legal sensitivity**
- **#20 MF Global:** Corzine was never criminally charged, and the CFTC case ended in a civil settlement. Say exactly that.
- **#27 Tricolor and First Brands:** the defendants deny the charges and these are pending criminal cases. Use "alleged" and "charged", and update on the docket before publishing.
- **#28 Hunt brothers:** use the civil verdict wording exactly.
- **#13 Funeral industry and #19 Vet bills:** company-specific pricing claims must come from filings or regulator findings, not activist videos.
- **#5 Prediction markets:** litigation is live and may reach the Supreme Court. Date-stamp the legal status in the script.

**Fast-moving numbers**
- **#1 Tariff refunds:** totals change weekly. Re-pull the CBP CAPE figures on script day.
- **#6 Social Security:** an updated Trustees Report would change the dates.
- **#3 Gold:** the market value moves daily. State the spot-price date.
- **#16 Delaware:** officials withheld some revenue data in 2026, so use the published DOF monthly reports only.
- **#18 Hormuz:** the war situation is ongoing. Avoid predictions and use EIA and IEA numbers only.

**Data gaps**
- **#4 IV fluids:** the "60%" figure originates with the American Hospital Association, not Baxter. Find a Baxter or FDA statement, or attribute it to the AHA.
- **#10 Sportsbooks:** parlay hold by bet type is published only by some states (e.g., Illinois, Pennsylvania). Pick states that publish it.
- **#15 Pallets:** Brambles reports pallets, crates and containers combined, so do not call them all "pallets".
- **#21 Puerto Rico:** the total debt figure is quoted variously ($70bn+, or about $120bn including pensions). Use FOMB or the court-confirmed figures.
- **#2 Penny:** use the Mint's FY2025 report if it has been published. The FY2024 report gives 3.69¢.

**Channel naming**
- Small YouTube channels named "Hidden Economics" and "Hidden Economy" already exist. Check title and search collisions when publishing.

**Overlaps to watch**
- #4 with Ep 29 (healthcare).
- #11 with Ep 04 (Norway fund).
- #18 with Ep 18 (helium as a chip gas).
- #24 with Ep 38 (airports and fees).
- Keep the angles distinct.
