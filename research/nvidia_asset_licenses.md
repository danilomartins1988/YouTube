## Colour note

The green accent (#7CD340) is chosen to suggest the "Nvidia ecosystem". It is deliberately **not** Nvidia's brand green, and no Nvidia, AMD, Google, Amazon, TSMC or other logo, product render, die shot or keynote image is used.

## Not used, and why

- **No photographs or press images.** The brief allows "appropriately licensed or officially provided" images. Nvidia's and TSMC's press galleries have usage terms (editorial use, no commercial implication) that could not be confirmed for this channel within the production, so none were used. Arbitrary web images were not assumed to be reusable.
- **No stock footage, watermarked media or AI-generated images.** No image-generation integration is authorised, and paid image services were not allowed.
- **Company names** appear only as plain text, in factual statements (nominative use). The film is independent and states that it is not affiliated with any company.
