## Colour note

Gold marks value and the Netherlands, violet-blue marks EUV light and technology, teal marks suppliers, and red is reserved for export restrictions. No ASML, Zeiss, Trumpf, Cymer, Intel, TSMC, Samsung or other logo, product render, machine photo or cleanroom image is used; the machine is a labelled schematic ("conceptual modules, not the physical layout").

## Not used, and why

- **No photographs or press images.** Company press galleries have editorial-use terms that could not be confirmed for this channel; arbitrary web images were not assumed to be reusable.
- **No stock footage, watermarked media or AI-generated images**, and no paid image or audio service.
- **No flags or official emblems.** Countries are shown only as neutral map outlines.
- **Company and government names** appear only as plain text, in factual statements (nominative use). The film is independent and is not affiliated with any company or government.
- **Currency.** ASML reports in euros; all amounts are shown in US dollars converted at the ECB 2025 average (1.13), stated on screen.
