## Linguistic notes (honest)
- **EN:** "Paw paw, paw paw, paw hooray!" keeps the 7-syllable rhythm of "Pa-ta, pa-ta, pa-ta-tá" and the owner's provisional EN signature ("Paw, paw, paw, hooray!"). Whisper hears it as "pa, pa… hooray" (it can't spell the sung "paw"); the owner should listen. "Jump/spin" use bare verbs after the name ("Pandora jumps, jump, jump, jump") on purpose for toddler repetition. "Ayra" is transcribed "Ira" (sung as "Ái-ra", fine).
- **ES:** neutral Latin American: "colita", "aplaude y zapatea", "vamos a bailar" (no vosotros, no Spain-only words). Whisper hears "aplauda" in the chorus (formal imperative) — possibly the singer's diction; owner/native check recommended. The ES chorus keeps "Pata, pata, patatá" (same Patati/Makeba risk as PT).
- **Sung-word verification:** PT and EN all lines verified by Whisper; ES Ayra/Toby/Clara verses were re-sung by inpainting (v2–v4) until Whisper confirmed the names and lyrics.
